Pell Promise for Qualified Students
Carroll College is expanding its Pell Promise to fully cover tuition for qualifying Pell-eligible first-time, first-year students from any state, as well as qualifying transfer students, beginning in fall 2027.
The expanded Pell Promise builds on the program Carroll launched for Montana students in 2024 and extends the opportunity to students nationwide. To qualify, students must be federal Pell Grant recipients and have a GPA of 3.0 or higher. Carroll will cover the remaining cost of tuition after applying the student’s federal Pell Grant, at least $3,500 in subsidized federal Stafford loans, and any additional institutional aid.
The Pell Promise is renewable each year for eligible students who continue to file the FAFSA, qualify for a federal Pell Grant, live in Carroll residence halls, and meet the program’s GPA and credit requirements.

Why is Carroll College Offering Pell Promise?
Clearer Cost and Removing Financial Barriers
The expansion is the latest step in Carroll’s broader effort to make the cost of college clearer and more accessible for students and families. Beginning in fall 2027, Carroll will reduce its published undergraduate tuition by 40 percent, from $43,260 to $26,800, and eliminate general undergraduate fees. The new tuition structure is designed to replace the traditional high-tuition, high-discount model with a more transparent starting price that more closely reflects what students and families actually pay.
Together, the tuition reset and expanded Pell Promise are designed to address two different barriers to college access: the perception created by a high published price and the financial gap that can remain for students with the greatest demonstrated need.
More Accessible to All Students and Families
The Pell Promise expansion and the new tuition model are part of a broader effort to make a Carroll education more accessible and the path to enrollment simpler for students and families. Carroll has simplified and accelerated its admission and financial aid processes, including free applications, faster admission decisions, earlier financial aid offers, test-optional admission, and fewer application requirements. The college has also taken steps to streamline the transfer process and has seen an increase in students seeking to transfer to Carroll. These efforts reflect Carroll’s continued focus on removing barriers and creating more pathways for students to pursue a Carroll education.
The federal Pell Grant Program provides need-based grants to undergraduate students with significant financial need. Eligibility is determined through the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA).

How to Qualify and Apply for Pell Promise
Steps to take advantage of Pell Promise
Apply to Carroll College as an entering freshman or transfer student.
File the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA). You must also qualify for the Pell Grant.
Review your aid offer and agree to borrow a $3,500 federal Stafford loan
Maintain satisfactory academic progress
Reside on-campus
Scholarship Eligibility Criteria
Residency: Must live in the United States.
Must reside on-campus.
Enrollment Status: Must be a first-time freshman or transfer student enrolling in an undergraduate program for the Fall of 2027.
FAFSA Requirement: Must complete the Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA).
Pell Grant Eligibility: Must qualify for a federal Pell Grant after filing the FAFSA.
Loan Acceptance: It is assumed students will accept the maximum subsidized Stafford Loan* included in the financial aid package.
Scholarship Composition: The scholarship package consists of a combination of federal aid, Carroll College merit aid, Carroll College need-based gift aid, and a maximum subsidized federal Stafford Loan.*
Academic Performance: Must maintain a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher.
Credit Load: Must enroll in and successfully complete at least 12 credits per semester.
Duration: Scholarship is valid for up to eight semesters.
Ongoing Eligibility: Must remain federal Pell Grant eligible each year to continue receiving the scholarship.
Coverage: Scholarship covers tuition only; external scholarships can be applied toward room and board.
Exclusions: Does not cover tuition for summer or winter sessions, study abroad programs, or course/orientation fees.
Additional Criteria: Other criteria and exceptions may apply, including considerations for athletic aid. Contact the Financial Aid Office (FAO) for detailed information.
* The maximum subsidized federal Stafford Loan is $3,500 freshman year, $4,500 sophomore year, and $5,500 junior and senior year.
