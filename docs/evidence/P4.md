## Verification implementation

`web/src/lib/verification.ts` provides a report-only P4 verification layer over the
accepted P2 model and pinned P1/P3 evidence. It contains:

- a CellData-aware audit that emits one receipt per metadata representation without
  deduplicating duplicate rich-text forms and preserves explicit offline methods;
- HTTP/target classification that distinguishes reachable, redirected, dead-target,
  and inconclusive states while making every network outcome insufficient for award
  closure;
- typed date parsing for exact datetimes, date-only values, Google Sheets serials,
  labeled and unlabeled ranges, open-only values, annual rules, rolling rules,
  month-only and approximate values, explicit unknowns, invalid calendar dates, and
  time zones;
- a fail-closed cycle engine in which invalid, unknown, annual-without-year,
  approximate, month-only, or unconfirmed values cannot become active;
- field freshness policies, direct-official acceptance gates, child/parent/provider/
  document/aggregator authority ordering, field-level evidence receipts, and conflict
  decisions; and
- deterministic manual-review severity receipts. These are fixture/workflow severities,
  not independent code-review findings.

Field authority and directness are derived from runtime-validated source bindings,
not asserted by individual claims. Each source binding produces a schema-valid P2
canonical Source and each field receipt embeds a schema-valid canonical Evidence item.
The evidence receipt hash binds the observed subject/field/value, canonical source,
relationship and subject scope, retrieval time, source update time, and source revision;
it intentionally excludes the caller's claim ID so duplicate observations collide and
fail closed. Every generated report also carries a SHA-256 over its complete
deterministic body.

## Rejected first candidate and remediation

The first frozen candidate
`2a9daa87b62d60e21d14ed6d4c639f6459ed40ce` and its uncommitted manifest
SHA-256 `ee8e919b05e6cd1a5a48e6717ae2173141dc7705bc880192c061456295d77370`
were independently rejected at C0/H4/M3/L0. They must never be attested or
delivered. The read-only reviewer found that:

- the exact verified-state candidate could not rerun its P4 generator because the
  generator accepted only an active P4 state;
- unresolved redirects and unknown successful application target types could fall
  through as reachable without review;
- an exact-datetime range with a future opening could be misclassified active;
- field claims could self-assert source authority/directness without runtime schema
  validation or canonical Source/Evidence binding;
- duplicate claims and receipts were not rejected inside the engine;
- fractional spreadsheet serials lost their time component; and
- an explicit Annual label was lost on year-qualified ranges.

The replacement remediation makes the generator valid across exact active, verified,
and accepted P4 topology; treats unresolved redirects and unknown targets as review;
checks exact open and close instants; runtime-validates fixtures and canonical
source/evidence bindings; rejects duplicate claim, source, and evidence identities;
preserves fractional serial wall time with an explicit timezone; and preserves an
explicit annual recurrence. Each boundary has a focused adversarial regression. The
same reviewer must recheck the frozen replacement before acceptance.

The first remediated replacement
`c26cbb94086601136bfcd3e5dc5ce77716296365` and its uncommitted manifest
SHA-256 `e020edad1d56429e125f21a2286c58f84a8f0e0bae23d826bd6da368cfd05853`
were independently rejected at C0/H3/M1/L0. They also must never be attested or
delivered. The reviewer found that the permanent P4 check would reject a later active
stage, source authority/directness could still be constructed from caller assertions,
DST-fallback fractional serials could choose one of two valid instants and falsely
close a cycle, and URL-equivalent aliases could escape source/evidence uniqueness.

The second remediation permits accepted P4 checks while a later stage is active;
requires canonical Source records plus matching classified authority and subject-link
Evidence before a field can become current/direct/official; treats ambiguous or
nonexistent local exact times as unknown; and normalizes canonical URL identity before
Source IDs and uniqueness. Adversarial regressions cover P5-active topology,
authority-label tampering, the `2026-11-01 01:30` Denver fallback ambiguity, and
default-port/host-case URL aliases. A new candidate and manifest are required.
