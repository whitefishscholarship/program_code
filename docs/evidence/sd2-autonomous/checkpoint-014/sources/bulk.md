The value of a good education cannot be overstated in its power to broaden minds and open windows of opportunity.

Regardless of the career field one feels called to pursue, having clear goals and a desire to create change is all that’s needed to embark on an impactful career. Unfortunately, beginning this path is difficult, coming at great expense and landing many young students in significant debt.

This scholarship seeks to support students who are pursuing higher education in order to make a marked difference in the world.

Any undergraduate or graduate student who has a vision for what you will be able to accomplish with your education may apply.

To apply, tell us what you want to build and how you hope this will help you and your community in the future.

Selection Criteria:

Ambition, Drive, Impact

Essay Topic Requirements

In 300-500 words, tell us about something you want to "build." This can be a physical product, a personal goal, a commitment, a relationship... how are you hoping to build your future? How will this make a positive impact on you and your community?
