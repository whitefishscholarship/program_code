# <PRIVATE_PERSON> All Montana Tribal Nations Scholarship

## Eligible Student

Graduating High School Seniors

## Application Open

Thursday, January 01, 2026

## Deadline Date

Tuesday, March 31, 2026

## Criteria

#### Required Criteria

The following are required criteria for this scholarship. Applicants _**must**_:

- be a member of one of the 8 Tribes in Montana. Applicant must provide documentation of enrollment.
- have a GPA of 2.25 or higher.
- provide proof of financial need.
- be enrolled, per the requirements of each scholarship, at an accredited postsecondary institution by the start of the first term of the awarded period.

#### Continuing Criteria

Scholarship recipients will be eligible to receive the awarded scholarship for up to 4 academic years. Current recipients of this scholarship looking to continue the scholarship for another year _**must**_:

- continue to meet the required criteria for this scholarship;
- submit their most recent transcript and proof of continuing enrollment; and
- complete the MCF Continued Scholarship Form (sent via email in early Spring to each recipient).

#### Reapplication

Previous recipients are NOT allowed to apply again.

## Awards

This scholarship typically provides the following each academic year.

#### Award Type

Multi-Year (Up to 4 Years Awarded)

#### Amount per Award

Typically **$2,500 to $3,000** per academic year.

#### \\# of Awards

Typically **3 to 4** recipients each year.
