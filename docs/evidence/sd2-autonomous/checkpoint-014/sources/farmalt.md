## MFBF Scholarship - Sponsored by the Women's Leadership Committee

The Montana Farm Bureau Federation will present two $1,500.00 scholarships made available through special efforts of the MFB Women’s Leadership Committee. The scholarships are available to both boys and girls. The winner will be selected on the following criteria: 1) Be in any grade in any post-secondary education, such as trade schools, college, etc. 2) Scholarship must be used the year it is awarded. 3) Must be a current paid Montana Farm Bureau member, or an immediate family member of a current paid Montana Farm Bureau member.

This application is due on April 1, 2026.

[Apply here!](https://form.jotform.com/250344617133147)
