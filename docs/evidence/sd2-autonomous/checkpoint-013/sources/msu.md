## [Can students accept both Montana University System (MUS) Scholarship and the Premier\  Scholarship?](https://www.montana.edu/admissions/scholarships/residents/\#collapsed84e515_d84e529)

The Premier Scholarship is specific to tuition only, so this scholarship cannot stack
with the MUS Honors Scholarship or the Presidential Scholarship. If you are granted
a Premier Scholarship and then receive the Presidential or MUS Honors Scholarship,
we will honor the award of the higher value.
