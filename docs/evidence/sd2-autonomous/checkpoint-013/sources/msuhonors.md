# The Honors College Presidential Scholarship

### The 2027 Presidential Scholarship is now **_open_**. It will close _**December 1, 2026**_ at midnight MST.

Presidential Scholarships are Montana State University's most distinguished academic
awards, recognizing scholastic achievement, demonstrated leadership, and unique personal
qualities. Scholarship recipients receive annual tuition waivers, a generous stipend,
and priority registration. Recent Presidential Scholars have gone on to receive renowned
awards such as the Goldwater, Truman, Rhodes, Marshall and Mitchell scholarships.
As ambassadors of the university, they reflect its highest ideals through demonstrated
leadership, public service, and academic excellence.  Montana residents and non residents
are eligible.

The Presidential Scholarship is a rigorous, competitive application, doubling as your
application to the Honors College at Montana State University. Recipients of the scholarship
routinely have a 3.9 non-weighted GPA or higher and are ranked in the top 3% of their
class.

Please review our [Frequently Asked Questions](https://www.montana.edu/honors/scholarships_faq.html) to determine if you are a competitive candidate for the scholarship.

All students interested in the Presidential Scholarship must complete the [MSU application](https://www.montana.edu/admissions/apply/) and be accepted to the university before completing the Presidential Scholarship
Application.

[Apply for the Presidential Scholarship](https://montana.academicworks.com/opportunities/30968)

### Eligibility Requirements

- Incoming freshman, first-time, first-year student
- High school non-weighted GPA of 3.7 or higher on a 4.0 scale


  - If you do not have a 3.7+ GPA, but feel you would be competitive for this scholarship,
     please contact the Honors College office at [contact redacted].
- Acceptance to MSU

### How To Apply

1. [Apply](https://www.montana.edu/admissions/apply/) and be accepted to MSU.
2. Claim your [NetID](https://www3.montana.edu/netidclaim/).
3. _Sign in_ to the [Cat$cholarships portal](https://montana.academicworks.com/) (gold button on the upper right corner of the screen).
4. Submit the General Scholarships Application _as soon as possible_. This must be complete to be considered for the Presidential Scholarship.
5. Access the Presidential Scholarship application and contact your recommenders.

1. Talk to one (1) high school counselor and two (2) recommenders. **_It is the applicant's responsibility to ensure that all materials, including letters_**
      **_or recommendation, are recieved by the deadline._**
2. Input the emails of the high school counselor and two recommenders into the Cat$cholarships
       portal and press "save". This sends your recommenders a link to complete their portion
       of the application.
3.  You can review your reference submissions under the recommendation tab on the Cat$cholarships
       portal home.
6. Complete the Presidential Scholarship Application. Return to complete or update the
    General Scholarships Application.
7. The portal will shut off at **_midnight Mountain Standard Time_**. Do _not_ wait until the last minute to submit.

### Selection Criteria

- Honors College Alignment
- Academic Excellence
- Leadership and Service

### Recommender Information

It is the responsibility of the student to ensure letters of recommendation are received
by the December 1st deadline. Transcripts and letters of recommendation will _not_ be accepted by mail or email. They **must** be uploaded to the Cat$cholarship application portal via the personal links counselors
and recommenders receive by email. Please check your spam before contacting our office.

### Scholarship Renewal

Presidential Scholars must maintain a 3.7 MSU GPA and complete an average of 16 MSU
credits per semester (32 MSU credits per year). Presidential Scholarships are renewable
for up to eight (8) academic semesters (excluding summer terms). Early graduation
results in forfeiting of unused semesters.
