## [What does “good standing” mean?](https://www.nationalhonorsociety.org/the-nhs-scholarship/scholarship-faq/\#block-what-does-good-standing-mean-content)

NHS seniors “in good standing” are active members of the current graduating class who are up to date on their NHS chapter obligations (GPA, service activities, meeting the chapter’s expectations for character and leadership). Students should refer to their NHS chapter’s bylaws for what constitutes “good standing” and check with their NHS advisers to confirm they meet all membership obligations.


## [What is an ideal candidate?](https://www.nationalhonorsociety.org/the-nhs-scholarship/scholarship-faq/\#block-what-is-an-ideal-candidate-content)

An ideal applicant for the NHS Scholarship will:

- Demonstrate a service mindset by seeking out and voluntarily fulfilling needs to help others.
- Commit to learning, growing, and making the most of educational opportunities.
- Take ownership and responsibility as a leader, in title or in action, in school and community.
- Display perseverance, honesty, integrity, and ethical and compassionate decision-making.


## [What will I be asked to provide in the scholarship application?](https://www.nationalhonorsociety.org/the-nhs-scholarship/scholarship-faq/\#block-what-will-i-be-asked-to-provide-in-the-scholarship-application-content)

While completing the application, applicants will be asked to:

- Provide detailed information about activities and experiences related to scholarship, service, leadership, and character.
- Request an Adviser Certification from their NHS Adviser, which acts as a short recommendation form and confirms the applicant is currently in good standing with a local NHS chapter. The adviser certification must be completed in the application portal and no external documents or communications will be accepted.
- Request a Recommendation from one non-adviser, non-family member adult who can speak to the applicant’s commitment to the pillars of NHS. The recommendation must be completed in the application portal and no external documents or communications will be accepted.


## [How do I select a great recommender?](https://www.nationalhonorsociety.org/the-nhs-scholarship/scholarship-faq/\#block-how-do-i-select-a-great-recommender-content)

Choose someone who knows you well and can speak to your character, leadership, growth, and impact. A strong recommendation comes from someone who has worked closely with you and can provide specific examples of your accomplishments and contributions.

If you remove your recommender and select a new one, make sure to respectfully inform your past recommender!

To help your recommender submit a strong recommendation form:

- Ask early and provide them with the submission deadline.
- Choose a teacher, counselor, coach, or mentor who knows you beyond grades alone. Your recommender cannot be your NHS adviser!
- Share your resume, activities, achievements, and scholarship goals.
- Remind them of specific projects, leadership experiences, or accomplishments they have observed.
- Confirm that the recommender’s email address entered in your application is correct and monitored regularly.
- Follow up respectfully if the recommendation form has not been completed as the deadline approaches.
- Thank them for their time and support.

**Tip:** A detailed recommendation from someone who knows you well is often stronger than a generic one from someone with a prestigious title.


## [What is the difference between verification and the NHS Adviser Certification?](https://www.nationalhonorsociety.org/the-nhs-scholarship/scholarship-faq/\#block-what-is-the-difference-between-verification-and-the-nhs-adviser-certification-content)

Account verification is necessary for any student who creates an account on the NHS website and can be completed by any NHS member at your school as soon as they are inducted. We recommend that students create accounts and get them verified by the adviser as early as possible so that they receive updates from the NHS national office. Verification allows the adviser to confirm membership of these students so that they can access these member-exclusive benefits and communications, including access to the NHS Scholarship. Students cannot log into the NHS Scholarship until they are verified.

If a verified NHS student decides to apply for the NHS Scholarship, they must enter the NHS Adviser Certification request into the scholarship platform. This allows the adviser to provide feedback on the individual student, including up-to-date reflections on standing and contributions to the school and chapter, which are not part of the basic account verification process.


## [When are scholarship recipients announced?](https://www.nationalhonorsociety.org/the-nhs-scholarship/scholarship-faq/\#block-when-are-scholarship-recipients-announced-content)

For the 2025-2026 cycle, scholarship recipients and chapter advisers were notified on February 4, 2026. Recipients must complete a Scholarship Acceptance Form in order to receive their award.

Applicants who were not selected also received an email notification on February 4. In the off chance that an applicant has not received an email update, that means they were not selected for an award.


## [How much are the individual awards this year?](https://www.nationalhonorsociety.org/the-nhs-scholarship/scholarship-faq/\#block-how-much-are-the-individual-awards-this-year-content)

- 575 National Semifinalists will receive $3,200.
- 24 National Finalists will receive $5,625 and an all-expense paid trip to DC for NELA.
- 1 National Finalist will receive $25,000 and an all-expense paid trip to DC for NELA.


## [How do winners receive their award payments?](https://www.nationalhonorsociety.org/the-nhs-scholarship/scholarship-faq/\#block-how-do-winners-receive-their-award-payments-content)

Scholarship checks are mailed to recipients’ college/university, and made payable to the designated institution in late July/early August. Checks or unused funds cannot be made payable to the individual recipient.


## [Is the NHS Scholarship renewable?](https://www.nationalhonorsociety.org/the-nhs-scholarship/scholarship-faq/\#block-is-the-nhs-scholarship-renewable-content)

The NHS Scholarship is a non-renewable award for undergraduate educational expenses.


## [Where can NHS Scholarship funds be used and what can they be used for?](https://www.nationalhonorsociety.org/the-nhs-scholarship/scholarship-faq/\#block-where-can-nhs-scholarship-funds-be-used-and-what-can-they-be-used-for-content)

Funds may be used at a U.S. college or university, military institute, or trade school towards qualified education expenses for undergraduate studies. Funds can be used for tuition and fees required for enrollment, as well as course-related expenses, such as fees, books, supplies, and equipment.


## [Are international students eligible?](https://www.nationalhonorsociety.org/the-nhs-scholarship/scholarship-faq/\#block-are-international-students-eligible-content)

Yes, international students may apply for the scholarship, but scholarship funds must be used at a U.S. college or university.
