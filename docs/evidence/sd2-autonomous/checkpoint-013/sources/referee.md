Welcome to the The Student Referee Scholarship for High School Student Referees enrolled in a state accredited sports officiating class or currently officiating games in their community.

Scholarship amounts are $500 (multiple scholarships to be awarded, dependent on donations)

Are you a dedicated student referee who balances the demands of academic life with the responsibilities of officiating in sports? Your commitment to fairness, leadership, and teamwork does not go unnoticed. The Student Referee Scholarship , we believe in recognizing and supporting individuals like you who contribute significantly to both their communities and their educational pursuits.

This scholarship is designed to reward exceptional student referees who exemplify integrity, dedication, and a passion for sportsmanship. By providing financial assistance, we aim to support your academic goals and celebrate the invaluable role you play in promoting positive athletic experiences. Whether you’re officiating a local game or leading a major tournament, your efforts make a meaningful difference.

We invite you to apply for the Student Referee Scholarship and share your journey as a student referee. Your unique perspective and dedication are what we seek to honor, and we are excited to support your continued growth and success both on and off the field.
