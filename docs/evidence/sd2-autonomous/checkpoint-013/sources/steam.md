Any student pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in a [STEAM](https://bold.org/scholarships/by-major/stem-scholarships/) field may apply for this scholarship if they’re a first or second-generation [immigrant](https://bold.org/scholarships/by-demographics/minorities/immigrants-scholarships/) or an otherwise underrepresented minority in higher education whose parents and grandparents haven’t attained the level of education being pursued.

To apply, tell us about your hopes and fears about pursuing college and how your background has affected your education and your goals.

Selection Criteria:

Ambition, Drive, Passion

Essay Topic Requirements

Write about your hopes or concerns about entering higher education as an outsider to the system. Specifically, how has your experience as a first or second-generation immigrant impacted your educational journey thus far, and how has it impacted your future goals?

400–600 words
