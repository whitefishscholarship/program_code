## \*NOW OPEN\*

### AVTRIP Scholarship: $2,000

### PURPOSE

Avfuel Corporation of Ann Arbor, Michigan, has established funds available for the purpose of furthering aviation education. These funds will be distributed under the name of the “AVTRIP Scholarship”.

#### The AVTRIP Scholarship

The AVTRIP Scholarship was designed to award one current AVTRIP member with funds to use toward furthering his or her aviation education. The scholarship will be in the amount of $2,000. These funds may be used for, but not limited to, the following areas: flight training, educational tuition fees, and a new license or rating.

A maximum of one AVTRIP Scholarship will be awarded for each calendar year.

[APPLY](https://form.jotform.com/260607434083151)

### APPLICATION DEADLINE

Complete applications will be due by **11:59 p.m. EDT on October 31st, 2026.**

### SELECTION CRITERIA

Avfuel Corporation will make the selection of the awarded recipients based solely on the answers of the application packet. Semi-finalists may be invited to a personal interview by staff of Avfuel Corporation and industry representatives who will make the final selection. The scholarship recipients will be notified by Avfuel.

### PAYMENT OF SCHOLARSHIPS

The scholarships will be distributed by check. The check will be made payable to the recipient and the educational institution of the recipient’s choice. The check must be endorsed by both, and must be used for the purposes of furthering the recipient’s aviation education. The 2026 scholarships must be redeemed by October 31, 2027.

Recipients must provide Avfuel Corporation with a detailed description of how the funds will be used.
