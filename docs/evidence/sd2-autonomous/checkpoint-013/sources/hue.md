Any [Asian American](https://bold.org/scholarships/by-demographics/minorities/asian-american-students-scholarships/) undergraduate student with volunteering experience may apply for this scholarship opportunity if they’re pursuing a career in the [mental health](https://bold.org/scholarships/by-demographics/mental-health-scholarships/) field. Applicants should exemplify advocacy and leadership, be engaged in uplifting mental health and/or disability rights, foster inclusivity, have personal experience with mental health or disability (personally, through one’s family, or through one’s community), think creatively, and maintain a strong academic record.

To apply, tell us why mental health and disability advocacy are so important in the Asian American community and how this scholarship will help you achieve all of your goals. Additionally, upload an image of you helping or upload a school portrait.

Selection Criteria:
