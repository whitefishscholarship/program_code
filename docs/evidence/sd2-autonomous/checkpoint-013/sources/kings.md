## Tips For a Winning Essay

1

### No AI Writing

All entries are checked with AI detection tools. Essays flagged as AI-generated are disqualified with no exceptions.

2

### Proofread Carefully

Check for typos and grammar issues. Not everything gets underlined in red in your editor.

3

### Stay On Topic

Essays that stray from the prompt are not considered, no matter how well written they are.

4

### Take a Position

The best essays make a specific argument and defend it. A summary of possibilities will not win.

5

### 500 Words Max

There is no minimum. A focused 400-word essay beats a padded 500-word one every time.
