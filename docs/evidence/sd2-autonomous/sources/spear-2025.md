Gladys R. Spear Memorial Scholarship
Whitefish High School
Our program provides a $500 scholarship to a Whitefish High School or Whitefish Independent High School graduating senior that has been diagnosed with a physical or learning disability.
We are pleased to invite applications from students pursuing life skills or post-secondary programs at vocation, technical, or trade schools, or undergraduate education.
Applicants must have a diagnosis of at least one of the following disabilities: autism spectrum, ADD/ADHD, Hearing Impaired, Blindness, Language Processing Disorder, Auditory Processing Disorder (APD), Non-verbal Learning Disabilities, Dyscalculia, Dysgraphia, Dyslexia, Visual Perceptual/Visual Motor Deficit, Behavioral Disorders or another that is not listed above.
Requirements:
Please write a brief essay on how you cope with your disability, challenges you have had to face and your goals for your future.
250 words
Attach two letters of recommendation from a family, friend, teacher or other professional individual.
