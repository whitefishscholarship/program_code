# Apply for the _Live Your Dream Award_

The [_Live Your Dream: Education and Training Award for Women_ ®](https://www.soroptimist.org/our-work/live-your-dream-awards/index.html) offers [three levels](https://www.soroptimist.org/our-work/live-your-dream-awards/apply-for-the-live-your-dream-awards.html#awardlevels) of cash awards. [Eligible applicants](https://www.soroptimist.org/our-work/live-your-dream-awards/apply-for-the-live-your-dream-awards.html#eligibility) can submit their application from **August 1 to November 15**. Ultimately, a _Live Your Dream Awards_ recipient has the potential to receive up to $16,000 to help offset tuition costs, purchase books, get transportation, or find reliable childcare so she can worry less about how to pay her bills and focus on achieving her education dreams.

[Access My Current Application](https://apply.soroptimist.org/acc/l/)

_The first level of judging occurs at the club level. Please note that each club has their own judging process, timeline, and award amount. Most clubs announce their recipients between January and April._

_Timelines to receive funds are dependent on your local club._

## Award Levels

###### Recipients have the potential to receive $1,000 - $16,000 to further their education:

### **$1,000**

Club-Level

Award amounts vary, but club-level recipients receive an average of $1,000 from a local Soroptimist club.

_Each club selects one first-place recipient to move on to the next level of judging._

### **$5,000**

Region-Level

Each club’s first-place recipient is eligible for a $3,000 or $5,000 region-level award.

_Each region selects one first-place recipient to move on to the next level of judging._

### **$10,000**

International-Level

Each region’s first-place recipient is eligible for one of three international-level awards.

#### Before going any further, make sure you are eligible. You are eligible to apply if you are a woman who:

- Provides the primary financial support for yourself and your dependents. Dependents can include children, spouse, partner, siblings and/or parents.
- Has financial need.
- Is enrolled in or has been accepted to a vocational/skills training or an undergraduate degree program.
- Is motivated to achieve your education and career goals.
- Resides in one of Soroptimist International of the Americas’ member countries/territories (Argentina, Belize, Bolivia, Brazil, Canada, Chile, Colombia, Costa Rica, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Guam, Honduras, Japan, Mexico, Nicaragua, Northern Mariana Islands, Panama, Paraguay, Peru, Philippines, Puerto Rico, South Korea, Taiwan, United States of America, Uruguay, Venezuela).
- Has not previously been the recipient of a Soroptimist _Women’s Opportunity_ or _Live Your Dream Award_.
- Does not have a graduate degree and is not pursuing one currently.
- Is not a Soroptimist member, an employee of Soroptimist International of the Americas or immediate family of either.
- Agrees to complete all written sections in your own words, reflecting your real experiences and goals. Artificial intelligence (AI) tools may help with grammar or organization, but your story must be your own.
