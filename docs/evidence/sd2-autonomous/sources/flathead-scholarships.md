### Flathead Electric Co-op Scholarships

Flathead Electric Cooperative’s scholarships are funded by unclaimed [capital credits.](https://www.flatheadelectric.com/about-my-co-op/what-are-capital-credits/) Despite the Co-op’s best efforts to return capital credits to members, many go unclaimed (people move and don’t provide a forwarding address, for example). As a not-for-profit electric cooperative, the state allows Flathead Electric to use capital credits that go unclaimed for five or more years for educational purposes in our service area, rather than returning them to the Montana General Fund. Flathead Electric Co-op offers over $120,000 in scholarships each year.

## FEC

Scholarship

Individual scholarships range from $1,500 to $6,000. The applicant or their parent/legal guardian must be a member of Flathead Electric Cooperative, served by the Co-op at their primary residence. Scholarship money must be used to attend an accredited post-secondary education institution in Montana. **Applications are due February 22, 2026.**

**Applications are now closed. Please check back next year.**

### Lineworker Scholarships

Flathead Electric Cooperative funds scholarships to assist students looking to become electrical lineworkers. Scholarship applicants must meet these requirements:

  - Student must be enrolled in the [Pre-Apprentice Line Program at Highlands College, Montana Technological University](https://mtech.edu/lineprogram/index.html) and fulfill all admission requirements.
  - Student’s hometown must be in the [service territory](https://www.flatheadelectric.com/about-my-co-op/how-were-governed/board-of-trustees/district-map/) of Flathead Electric Co-op. This includes:
    - Kalispell
    - Whitefish
    - Bigfork
    - Columbia Falls
    - Libby
    - Cooke City
  - Upon admission to the Pre-Apprentice Line Program, Montana Tech will invite students from Flathead Electric Co-op’s service area to apply for the scholarship and submit an essay on their background, career goals, and financial need.

## Lineworker

Scholarships

Two $5,000 scholarships are available annually for local students who want to attend Montana Tech’s Pre-Apprentice Line Program. Flathead Electric Cooperative funds these scholarships with unclaimed capital credit dollars, but Montana Technological University administers the scholarship.

[Learn More at MTech.edu](https://mtech.edu/lineprogram/index.html)

### Basin & MECA Scholarships

The Basin Electric Power Cooperative (BEPC) and Montana Electric Cooperatives’ Association (MECA) scholarship opportunities are provided by organizations associated with your Co-op. We’ve combined these applications for your convenience, allowing you to use one application to apply for both scholarships simultaneously. **Applications are due January 11, 2026.**

## BEPC

Scholarship

Basin Electric Power Cooperative (BEPC) Scholarship Program will award one $2,500 scholarship each year to a Flathead Electric Cooperative member’s child graduating from high school or currently attending an undergraduate college.

## MECA

Scholarship

The Montana Electric Cooperatives Association (MECA) Scholarship Program will award one $500 scholarship to a regional electric cooperative member’s child graduating from high school or currently attending an undergraduate college.

**Applications are now closed. Please check back next year.**
