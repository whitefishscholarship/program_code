GLADY R. SPEAR MEMORIAL SCHOLARSHIP
Whitefish High School 2026
The Gladys R. Spear Memorial Scholarship proudly awards a $500 scholarship to a
graduate of Whitefish High School or Whitefish Independent High School (Class of 2026)
who has been diagnosed with a physical or learning disability. We warmly invite
applications from students pursuing life skills or continuing their education through
vocational, technical, trade, or undergraduate programs.
This scholarship honors the strength, determination, and resilience of students who meet
life’s challenges with courage. Like Gladys R. Spear, applicants are individuals who refuse
to be defined by their disabilities. Instead, they rise above obstacles, embrace their
unique journeys, and strive to create meaningful and fulfilling futures.
Recipients of this scholarship embody perseverance, self-motivation, and a commitment
to growth—demonstrating that challenges can be transformed into powerful sources of
strength.
Eligible applicants must have a documented diagnosis of one or more of the following:
autism, ADD/ADHD, hearing impairment, blindness, language processing disorder,
auditory processing disorder (APD), nonverbal learning disabilities, dyscalculia,
dysgraphia, dyslexia, visual perceptual or visual motor deficits, behavioral disorders, or
another comparable condition not listed above.
