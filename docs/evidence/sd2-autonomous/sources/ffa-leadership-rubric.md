## OVERVIEW OF SCORING

**DIRECTIONS.** The scholarship selection committee will score each section individually. Sections are scored on a
scale from one (1) to five (5) based upon criteria outlined in each section. This scale represents the raw points available.
The raw score is then multiplied by a weighted number, which varies in each section, to produce the total score
for the section (**bolded** box at bottom of far-right column). The total scores from each section are then summed
to produce an overall total score for the application. The total score for the application is not necessarily revealing of
the final ranking of the applicant as compared to the scores of other applications. The purpose of assigning a score is
to provide an indicator so that the selection committee may consider special circumstances and adjust the final
rankings as needed.
