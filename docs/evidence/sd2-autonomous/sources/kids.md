## New sCHOLARSHIPS

### Eligibility

- Dependent of a parent or guardian who sustained a serious work-related injury or fatality.
- Between 16-26 years of age.
- Must have a high school diploma, GED, HiSet, or be a high school student in good standing (who will graduate in the current academic year).
- Proceeds must be used for tuition, fees, room and board, books, and required supplies and will be paid directly to the educational institution.
- Scholarship awards are contingent on the availability of funds and may be renewed
- FAFSA Not Required

### Submission Requirements

-  Submission of complete application and responses to essay questions
- A transcript of the most recent grades (Unofficial transcript is okay)

### Application Deadline: **March 1, 2026**

## RENEWAL Scholarship

### Eligibility

- Current or past recipient of a Kids’ Chance of Montana Scholarship
- Dependent of a parent or guardian who sustained a serious work-related injury or fatality.
- Between 16-26 years of age.
- Proceeds must be used for tuition, fees, room and board, books, and required supplies and will be paid directly to the educational institution.
- Scholarship awards are contingent on the availability of funds
- Scholarship eligibility is limited to 4 years.

### Submission Requirements

- Submission of complete renewal application
- Essay responses

### Application Deadline: **March 1, 2026**
