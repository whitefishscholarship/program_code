## Future Farmers of America (FFA)

[Apply Now\\
\\
(opens in a new tab)](https://montanaffa.org/applications/)

- This program is open to seniors graduating from high schools in Montana and graduates of those high schools who are enrolled as freshmen at a college or vocational school in any state and planning to major in a field related to the horticultural or arboriculture  industry.
- Applicants must be members of FFA and have completed at least 2 years of agricultural education classes.
- They must have a GPA of 2.0 or higher.
- Selection is based on academic achievement (10 points); FFA leadership, including agricultural education courses completed (15 points); an essay describing their career objectives and why they have selected a career or vocation in agriculture (15 points); their FFA Supervised Agricultural Experience (SAE) project (20 points); an essay on their community service and their role in those activities (10 points); financial need (5 points); a letter of reference from their adviser (5 points), and a letter of reference from a community member (5 points).

## 4H

- Purpose: The 4-H Scholarship, established by AMTOPP in 2012, supports Montana 4-H members (ages 9-19) in beautification projects involving landscape or arboriculture, fostering interest and skills in the 'greens industry.'
- Eligible Projects: Funded projects may include starting a community garden, installing native gardens in local parks, planting trees to combat invasive species, or creating a spirit garden.
- Eligibility Requirements: Applicants must be current Montana 4-H members and between the ages of 9 and 19.
- Application Deadline: March 13, 2026
