# Clerk & Recorder Scholarship

Each year, the Montana Association of Clerks and Recorders offers a scholarship for a graduating Flathead County high school senior continuing their education in Montana.


## About the Scholarship

The Montana Association of Clerks and Recorders sponsors this scholarship for a graduating high school senior who will attend an in-state college or university. Each year, one winner and one alternate are selected and notified.

The scholarship is paid directly to the school the student chooses, after the student has been accepted.

## What the Application Asks For

The application asks you to share:

- Your career objectives and future goals
- Any unusual family or personal circumstances that have influenced your life
- Your work experience
- Your school and community activities

## How to Apply

Pick up an application at the Clerk & Recorder's office, or download the current year's application from the notice above.

Return your completed application to:

**Flathead County Clerk & Recorder**
