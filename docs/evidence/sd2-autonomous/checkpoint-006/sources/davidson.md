# D.A. Davidson Memorial Scholarship

## Eligible Student

Current College Students, Graduating High School Seniors

## Application Open

Thursday, January 01, 2026

## Deadline Date

Tuesday, March 31, 2026


## Criteria

#### Required Criteria

The following are required criteria for this scholarship. Applicants _**must**_:

- be an employee of D.A. Davidson or a child (biological, adopted, step children) of a D.A. Davidson employee. Employee must have been employed at D.A. Davidson for a minimum of one-year (at the time the first award check is issued), and parent must work at least 30-hrs a week.
- demonstrate a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.5, per their most recent transcript (high school or college).
- be at least a part-time student (minimum of 6 credits).
- demonstrate academic achievement.
- be enrolled, per the requirements of this scholarship, at an accredited postsecondary institution by the start of the first term of the awarded period.

Applications will be evaluated based upon the applicant's ability to demonstrate and discuss academic achievement, financial need, long-term goals and objectives, and their participation in community activities through requested documents and essay question responses.

#### Reapplication

Previous recipients are NOT allowed to apply again.

## Awards

This scholarship typically provides the following each academic year.

#### Award Type

One-Time

#### Amount per Award

Typically **$500 to $1,500** per academic year.

#### \\# of Awards

Typically **5 to 10** recipients each year.
