COLLEGE SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM
The Flathead Conservation District (FCD) offers college scholarships to qualified local high school seniors.
ELIGIBILITY:
    •   Student must intend to pursue a degree in natural resources or related field.
        Note: Appropriate courses of study may include agricultural sciences, animal/range science,
        forestry, environmental science or engineering, hydrology, land resources, plant science, wildlife
        biology, etc.
    •   Student must reside in Flathead County.
    •   Student must have a minimum grade point average of 2.5.
    •   Students may receive a scholarship only once.
REQUIRED APPLICATION MATERIALS:
    •   A letter of recommendation from an instructor familiar with your work.
    •   Your official school transcript listing courses taken and grades received (all personal information
        will be kept confidential).
    •   An original 500-word essay answering ONE of the following questions:
        1. Describe yourself and your interest in natural resources (or related field of study). Why have
           you chosen this field? Describe your experience or community service pertaining to natural
           resources (e.g., agricultural experience, camps, Envirothon, coursework, FFA, 4-H, etc.).
        2. Describe what you believe to be the most important issue related to natural resources in
           your community and why. How do you plan to impact natural resources conservation in the
           future?
        Note: We encourage applicants to visit our website (www.flatheadcd.org) and other
        conservation district/organization websites to gain an understanding of what conservation is
        and the kinds of activities that support it.
OTHER INFORMATION:
    •   Scholarship awardees are required to attend a FCD board meeting to meet and talk with the
        Board of Supervisors about their education plans/goals.
    •   FCD will mail all scholarship awardees a form requesting additional information, which is
        required to process the award. The completed form must be submitted to the FCD office by
        June 1st.
    •   Payment will be approved by the board in June and the check will be sent directly to the school
        you will be attending. Failure to submit required forms may result in scholarship forfeiture.
DEADLINE:
   • Completed applications and required attachments must be received by March 13 at 4:00PM.
