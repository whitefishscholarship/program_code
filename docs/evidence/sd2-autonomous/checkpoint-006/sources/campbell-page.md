## About the <PRIVATE_PERSON> Scholarship

The <PRIVATE_PERSON> Scholarship Fund was established at Whitefish Community Foundation in 2022. One $2,500 scholarship is awarded annually to a student who is a graduate of high school in Flathead County, Montana or Lincoln County, Montana and intends to enroll in a post-secondary educational institution in the coming fall.  Applications are accepted January 30 to March 31, 2026 at 11:59 p.m.
