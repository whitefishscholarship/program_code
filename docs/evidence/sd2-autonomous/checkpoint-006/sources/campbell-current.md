DOROTHY AND LUTHER CAMPBELL SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM

The Dorothy and Luther Campbell Scholarship Program will award an annual
scholarship of $2,500 to support tuition and related expenses of the successful
applicant. The scholarship program will field applications every year, and the
scholarship may be awarded to the same applicant for up to four (4) consecutive years.


APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS & ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:
The application deadline is March 31, 2026 at 11:59 MDT. Late or incomplete
applications will not be accepted. Awards will be announced in May, 2026.


Applications must be submitted online. The online application is available during the
application window by logging into Whitefish Community Foundation’s scholarship portal
at: https://www.grantinterface.com/Home/Logon?urlkey=wcfscholarship


One successful candidate will be selected based on the following criteria:
1. Applicant criteria:
  •      Applicant must be a graduating senior in Flathead County or Lincoln County,
         Montana or have graduated from a high school in Flathead County or Lincoln
         County, Montana within three years of application date.

  •      Applicants must show evidence of a minimum G.P.A. of 3.0. (Transcript will be
         uploaded with application.)

  •      Preference will be given to individuals with a proven history of community
         involvement/volunteerism and a strong work ethic.

  •      Intend to enroll at a post-secondary educational institution in the fall of 2026 (all
         scholarships are paid directly to the institution upon verification of enrollment).

  •      Submit a completed application by the application deadline.

2. The application will include:
  •     One letter of recommendation uploaded by a current or former teacher, advisor,
        or coach written in the academic year the application is submitted. (NOTE:
        Letter of recommendation will be uploaded by the person providing the
        reference, not by the applicant. Reference providers must upload their
        letters by the application deadline.)

  •     One letter of recommendation uploaded by a current or former employer or
        community involvement supervisor written in the academic year the application
        is submitted. (NOTE: Letter of recommendation will be uploaded by the
        person providing the reference, not by the applicant. Reference providers
        must upload their letters by the application deadline.)

  •     Most current high school or college transcript.

  •     The following essay questions:
              i. Please describe a challenge you are overcoming as you pursue your
                 goal of attending a post-secondary educational institution.
             ii. Please describe any special attributes or accomplishments that set you
                 apart from the crowd.
            iii. Please explain what you believe you are capable of achieving once
                 you have completed your post high-school education. You may explain
                 this in terms of your life goals, career goals, and/or goals within your
                 community.
            iv. Describe volunteer activities in which you have chosen to participate,
                 why you chose those particular volunteer activities, and how those
                 experiences have affected your life.
