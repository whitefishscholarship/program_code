TRACI STOLTE MEMORIAL SCHOLARSHIP

                                    Provided by the Whitefish Lions Club


Number of Scholarships: Two
Amount: $1,000
Due Date: Please submit applications by March 30, 2026
Eligibility: Recipients will be selected based on volunteer service & essay.
Open to Whitefish High School graduating seniors who are interested in advancing their education by attending
a 4-year, 2-year, technical or vocational school. This includes training such as a journeyman certification.
Please submit volunteer service in resume form including dates, location, supervisor contact information,
description of volunteer service, etc.
Please submit TWO short essays. Essay quality is a determining factor in selection of recipients. Please
have someone review your essay for neatness, correct grammar and responses to prompt.
In the first essay please provide up to 300 words highlighting your volunteer experience, specifically addressing
the following:
       1.) Why did you choose to volunteer?
       2.) What did you expect when you chose the area you volunteered with?
       3.) How was your experience and what would you do to improve the experience for other volunteers?
In the second essay please provide up to 300 words on one of the following three prompts.
Essay Prompts:
       1.) What do you feel non-profit/volunteer organizations contribute to your community? Have any of
           these organizations had a direct impact on you?

       2.) Describe a situation when you had to ask someone for help, and describe the outcome. Did you get
           the help you needed? Was it different from what you expected?

       3.) How would you practice the ideals presented in this scholarship?


* PAYMENT OF SCHOLARSHIP: Money awarded for the scholarship will be paid to the recipient’s
college, university or institute. Institution information and student’s ID number will be requested.
