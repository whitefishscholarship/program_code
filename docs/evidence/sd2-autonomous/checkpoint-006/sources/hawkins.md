2026 Hawkins Scholarship Application
Hawkins is a scholarship for graduating seniors of accredited high schools in Flathead County. Thank you for wanting to continue your education.

The applicant must have a 2.5 GPA or higher.  Preference will be given for students who are both in financial need and worthy of the award.

INCOMPLETE APPLICATIONS WILL NOT BE ACCEPTED.  LATE APPLICATIONS WILL NOT BE ACCEPTED.

The recipient must not be related in any degree to any of the trustors, their heirs, or descendants.

All applicants will be given equal consideration.

In general, no person shall receive a grant or scholarship for more than a single year in   order that more young people may be assisted and encouraged to enter college.

The scholarship is paid in one installment, fall semester during the freshman year of college only.  Waivers/deferrals may be granted on a case-by-case basis.

A Hawkins grant may be forfeited if the recipient fails to maintain grades, does not carry a full-time credit load throughout the grading period, does not enroll as a full-time student, or engages in habits or conduct unbecoming to himself/herself, the college, or the Hawkins Foundation.

The 2025 application packet must be submitted to your counselor/ Career Center by Wednesday, March 18, 2026
