# Junior Scholarships

## Learn more about scholarship opportunities via the MSGA!

[MSGA Scholarship Application](https://www.msgagolf.org/juniors/scholarships/msga-scholarship-application)


# The MSGA scholarship is awarded to a graduating senior who has been involved in golf. This involvement may include participation on a high school team or in a junior program and need not be presently participating. This scholarship is NOT based on golf skill. Multiple scholarships are usually awarded.

**The Scholarship Application deadline for 2026 was March 15th.**

**Selected recipients will be notified soon. Please check again in the fall for 2027's application!**
