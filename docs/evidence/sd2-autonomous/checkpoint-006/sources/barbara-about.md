How to Apply
The Barbara Mansfield Champion Scholar Award is currently accepting applications. The deadline
for submitting applications is March 20. The winning recipients will be notified in May. Disbursements
will be paid directly to the educational institution that the student plans to attend.

Qualifications:
To qualify for this scholar award, you must be a woman who:
    • Is completing or has completed high school at Whitefish, Glacier, or another designated Flathead
        Valley high school.
    • Has a minimum 3.2 GPA.
    • Is pursuing further education and enrolling full-time in a college, university or trade school starting
        in the fall.
    • Must demonstrate financial need based on your FAFSA results and Student Aid Index (SAI) score


*There is no age restriction on applicants. Women who are Black, Indigenous, or a woman of color are
strongly encouraged to apply.


The Application:
The application for the Barbara Mansfield Champion Scholar Award has two parts: the application form and
a personal statement (described on the following page). The PDF application is electronically editable;
however, it can also be printed and filled in by hand. The personal statement must be typed or submitted
as a video. To be considered for the scholar award, the application form and the personal statement need
to be submitted to [contact omitted] by the March 20 deadline. Incomplete
applications will not be accepted.

                                                                         The Last Best Ride
The personal statement:
Barbara Mansfield is a champion educator who pushed students to discover and develop their passions,
skills, and academic potential throughout her career.

The Barbara Mansfield Champion Scholar Award recognizes that champions come in many shapes and
forms, including but not limited to athletic champions, champions for equal rights, champions of the arts or
the environment. But no matter the cause, every champion has similar characteristics: courage, passion,
strength, and persistence. Those qualities are not always innate. Champions can develop those
characteristics as they overcome challenges and work to achieve goals.

Prompt: Please craft a personal statement that describes a time when you overcame a significant
challenge or set a significant goal and achieved it. What skills or qualities did you use to overcome that
challenge or to achieve that goal? How do you think your story embodies being a champion?

You can submit your response in either a written or video format:
   • Written personal statement: Please keep your responses to 400-600 words in length.
       Submissions should be typed and will be evaluated on the quality of the response, as well as
       spelling, grammar and punctuation.
   • Video personal statement: Please keep your responses to 3-5 minutes in length. Submissions
       will be evaluated on the quality of the response, including public speaking skills and clarity of
       communication. Videos should be submitted in a MP4 format.

Additional, but not required: Some students have a background, talent, interest, or way they personally
identify that’s important to them or their identity. They don’t feel their application would be complete without
it. If this sounds like you, please share as an addition to your personal statement (100 words for written
option or 1 minute for video option).

How to Submit the Application
Completed applications should be submitted via a single email containing the application form and personal
statement (written or video) to [contact omitted]. Applications will be accepted
until 11:59 p.m. PT on March 20. Incomplete applications will not be accepted.

Any questions on the application or essay can also be submitted to the same email address. We will
respond to your question as soon as possible.

Application Evaluation
A diverse group of professionals will evaluate all applications. Applications will be reviewed by late April,
and a decision on the scholar award recipients will be made in early May. Disbursements will be paid directly
to the educational institution that the student plans to attend.
