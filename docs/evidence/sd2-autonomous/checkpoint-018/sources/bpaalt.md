# Scholarships

**Business Professionals of America** partners with generous organizations to offer scholarships that support students’ educational goals.

Each year, scholarships are awarded to outstanding Secondary and Post-secondary members who demonstrate academic excellence and a strong commitment to BPA. With help from our partners and donors, many members take the next step in their education journey.

## Available Scholarships

Applications must be submitted by **5 p.m. ET on March 15 of each Membership Year.**
