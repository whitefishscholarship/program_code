### Eligibility

The following are the requirements to be eligible to apply for the Blue Collar Brain scholarship program.

- Must be a **United States citizen** or have a **student visa**
- Must be a **high school graduate** or recipient of a **GED**
- Must be enrolled or plan to enroll **full-time** in a **vocational/technical school or 2-year college** for the fall 2025 semester
- Plan to acquire certification or degree in a **skilled trade program**\*
- **Proof of enrollment** in an accredited school is required
- **GPA of 3.0** or higher from the last school attended

\\* Skilled trade programs include (but are not limited to) automotive, aviation, carpentry, construction, electrical, heavy equipment, HVAC, machinery, manufacturing, plumbing and welding. Blue Collar Brain reserves the right to exclude an application that is not defined as a trade specific educational program.

### How To Apply
