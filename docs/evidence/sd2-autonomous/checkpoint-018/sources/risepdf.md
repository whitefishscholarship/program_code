## APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS AND ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:

**The application deadline is March 31, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. MDT. Late applications will not be
accepted.** Awards will be announced in May, 2026.

Applications must be submitted online. The online application is available during the application
window by logging into Whitefish Community Foundation’s scholarship portal at:
[portal locator withheld; exact query hash in receipt].

Applicants must meet the following requirements to be eligible for scholarship consideration:

• Be a current employee of a business entity associated with the Glacier Restaurant Group
R.I.S.E. Scholarship Program or have been employed by Glacier Restaurant Group within
one year prior to the date of the application, OR be a child or legal dependent of an
eligible employee of a business entity associated with the Glacier Restaurant – R.I.S.E.
Scholarship Program. The eligible employee must be employed by the employer at the
time of application.

---

• Have earned a high school or current college G.P.A. of 3.5 or higher (high school or
college transcripts must be uploaded with application).

• Provide a letter of recommendation from a current or former
teacher/professor/instructor written within two years of the application **and** a letter of
recommendation from a current or former employer or community involvement
supervisor written within two years of the application. (NOTE:  **Letters of
recommendation will be uploaded by the person providing the reference, not by the
applicant. Reference providers must upload their letters by the application deadline.)**

• Three essays written by the applicant addressing the following topics:

• Special attribute(s) or accomplishment(s) that sets applicant apart.

• Brief description of applicant’s short-term/long-term goals and how their
college education pertains to those goals.

• Description of volunteer activities in which applicant has chosen to participate,
why applicant chose those particular volunteer activities, and how those
experiences have affected applicant’s life.

• Intend to enroll at a post-secondary educational institution in the fall of 2026 (all
scholarships are paid directly to the institution upon verification of enrollment).

• Submit a completed application by the application deadline.
