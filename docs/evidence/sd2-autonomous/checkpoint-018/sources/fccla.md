Each year, Family, Career and Community Leaders of America (FCCLA), along with generous partners and sponsors, awards scholarships to recognize outstanding members who demonstrate leadership, achievement, and commitment to the organization’s mission.

#### FCCLA Unified Scholarship Application

FCCLA has made it easier than ever for members to apply for one or more of these scholarship opportunities through the **FCCLA Unified Scholarship Application**. This single application streamlines the process, allowing applicants to select whether they would like to apply for one, several, or all of the scholarships listed below, without the need to submit multiple separate applications.

FCCLA has introduced a scholarship rubric to assist members through the application process. The rubric outlines key criteria and expectations to help applicants submit their strongest possible application. Be sure to use the new scholarship rubric when completing your application!

* * *

### How To Apply

- To apply, FCCLA Members must log in to their **FCCLA Member Portal** using their unique **Member ID Number**.
- Once logged in, navigate to the **Surveys & Applications** tab and select **“Scholarships”** from the dropdown menu to access the **FCCLA Unified Scholarship Application**.

**The application deadline is March 1, 2027, at 11:59 PM ET.**


- If you experience any issues accessing your Member Portal, please first contact your **Chapter Adviser**. If you need additional assistance accessing your member account, please email your [State Adviser.](https://fcclainc.org/about/state-associations)

## National Technical Honor Society Scholarship

The National Technical Honor Society (NTHS) Scholarship recognizes three outstanding FCCLA Members who demonstrate exceptional leadership, service, and commitment within both FCCLA and the National Technical Honor Society.

**[image omitted; uninspected]About the National Technical Honor Society:**

The National Technical Honor Society honors student achievement and leadership in Career and Technical Education. Partnering with FCCLA, NTHS celebrates students who exemplify excellence in academics, community involvement, and professional skill development across Family and Consumer Sciences education.

### **Selection Criteria**

Recipients will be selected based on their remarkable contributions and leadership within FCCLA and the National Technical Honor Society.

### **Scholarship Award**

Three recipients will each receive a **$1,000** scholarship award, which will be directly applied to their college or university tuition.

### **Application Deadline**

The application deadline is **March 1, 2027, at 11:59 PM ET**.
