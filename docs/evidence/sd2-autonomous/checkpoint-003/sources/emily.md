Emily Leighty/DKG Lambda Scholarship Overview


Important Dates

•      Applications due by 4/3 to WHS Guidance Office (WHS Guidance Office)



Scholarship Details:

•      Application format: typed
•      Amount: $1,000
•      Eligibility: Open to female high school seniors who are enrolled at Whitefish High
       School who aspire to pursue a major in Education/working with children. The
       scholarship must be used in the year it was awarded.



Application Requirements:

    ● Application form

    ● Transcript of grades and credit

    ● Copy of acceptance letter to an accredited institution of higher learning

    ● Statement of intent to pursue a degree and career in education/working with children
       (teaching, administrator, counseling, etc.)
    ● List of extracurricular interest/activities (this can be in the form of a resume as well)

    ● 2 letters of recommendation
