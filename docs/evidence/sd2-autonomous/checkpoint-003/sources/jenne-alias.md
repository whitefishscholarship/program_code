Montana State Elks Association, Inc.
   William and Sara Jenne' Scholarship Application Form
 Requirements: This Scholarship is intended for students who have completed their first year of
instruction at a Montana institution of higher learning and intend to continue their education into their
second year of study at a Montana school. Applicants must be able to demonstrate the completion of
full year of study and cumulative GPA of 2.0 to be considered.
This application and any attachments become the property of the Montana State Elks Association and
will not be returned to the applicant. The application must be filled out completely. Attachments may be
included that amplify and support the application. Care should be taken not to include unnecessary
information.
Applications must be mailed no later than June 1st and winners will be notified no later than August
15th of the same year and will be given instructions at that time on how to collect the award.
