# Lonnie Collinsworth Scholarship
Scholarship Details:

Amount: $1,500, non-renewable

Eligibility:

   ●   Open to high school seniors enrolled at Whitefish High School

   ●   Must be a Montana resident

   ●   Pursuing further education at either a traditional 4-year, or a 2-year school either
       in-state, or out-of-state

   ●   There is no restriction regarding major/area of study

   ●   Minimum 3.0 GPA

   ●   Minimum 1 Year of Art in High School
