The scholarship was established by Jane Fellows, in memory of her mother, Margaret
Fellows. The name of Jane Fellows was added to the scholarship by Whitefish Theatre
Company (WTC) in honor of her dedicated service as Artistic Director of WTC.

East year, WTC presents this award to provide funds for the furthering of the recipient’s
education. The applicant must have participated in some aspect of theatre with special
consideration to those who have participated in WTC programs.

This scholarship is not based on financial need. This scholarship is sent directly to the
recipient’s school.

Each applicant must:
   1. Make formal written application for the scholarship. Turn into [named recipient omitted] by
      4/30/2026.
   2. Attach a resume and school transcript to the application.
