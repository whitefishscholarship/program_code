Whitefish Education Foundation-Community Service
Scholarship Overview


Important Dates

•      Applications due by 4/4 to WHS Guidance Office (WHS Guidance Office)



Scholarship Details:

•      Application format: typed
•      Amount: $1,000 (2)



Eligibility:

   Available to WHS graduating seniors planning to attend an accredited post-secondary
    institution.
   Students must have participated in community service or other volunteerism during their
    high school years. This could range from volunteering at a non-profit organization to
    assisting an elderly neighbor.



Application Requirements:

   Application form
   Transcript of grades and credit
   Completion of an essay with the prompt, “How I have impacted my community through
    volunteerism and what I’ve learned through my experience.” (250 words)



PAYMENT OF SCHOLARSHIP: Money awarded for the scholarship will be paid to the
recipient’s college, university, or institute in July/August of the year awarded/ Institution and
student’s ID # will be requested.
