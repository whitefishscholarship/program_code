P.E.O. STAR Scholarship
The P.E.O. STAR Scholarship awards scholarships to high-achieving young women
who are in their final year of high school and plan to attend an accredited
postsecondary educational institution in the United States or Canada in the
next academic year.




A one-time $2,500 scholarship is available to women, recommended by a P.E.O. chapter, who exhibit excellence in leadership, academics,
extracurricular activities, community service and potential for future success.
