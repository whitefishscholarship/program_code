The John L. & Ann Taylor Scholarship Trust Application
      Administered by: D.A. Davidson Trust Company (contact values omitted)

CRITERIA FOR SCHOLARSHIP
       1. Must be a graduating senior from an accredited high school in the Flathead Valley in
          the State of Montana with at least a 3.0 GPA.
       2. Must be seeking a Bachelor or Master’s degree of Fine Arts, with an emphasis on painting, oil and
          watercolor, and printing using these stated mediums.

1. COVER SHEET
Cover sheet must include:
          A. Scholarship applicant’s name
          B. Applicants address, email, and phone number
          C. Applicants GPA both current and cumulative
          D. Applicants school history. Include which elementary, middle and high school the applicant has
             attended (please include city and state of the school).
          E. List the colleges to which you have been accepted (list in order of priority)
                 a. List the approximate cost of each (tuition, room & board, books etc.)
          F. College major
          G. State Career Goals in 50 words or less

2. HIGH SCHOOL RESUME
Please attach your information pertaining to each category in the following format:
      Academic Honors
      Year                  Honor
      Leadership Experience
      Year                  Activity            Position Held
      Extracurricular and Personal Activities
      Year                  Activity            Total Hours

3. PERSONAL STATEMENT
       Describe why a college degree in the fine arts is very important to you and include a statement of
       financial need. (300 words or less)

4. HIGH SCHOOL TRANSCRIPT
       Attach an official high school transcript listing classes and grades through the seventh semester.

5. LETTER OF REFERENCE
       Attach one letter of reference from a high school art teacher which addresses the following:
           A. Overview of the teacher’s high school experience with applicant.
           B. An evaluation of the student’s art portfolio, including: number of pieces, type of art, quality of
              artwork, and skill level.
           C. The likelihood of applicant successfully completing a Bachelor or Master’s of Fine Arts Degree.
           D. Letter should be printed on Letterhead and signed.

6. ART PORTFOLIO
       Copies of your art portfolio would be appreciated. Please limit the art to 6 pieces. Please also
       provide a brief description of the art pieces, including the medium used.

DEADLINE AND SUBMISSION INFORMATION
       1. All applications become the property of the John L. and Ann Taylor Scholarship
         Committee, and will not be returned.
       2. Return completed applications to the D.A. Davidson Trust Company (see address at top)
       3. DEADLINE: April 10th
