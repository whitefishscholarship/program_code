Jack Quatman Scholarship Overview
Important Dates

● Applications due by 4/4 to WHS Guidance Office (WHS Guidance Office)

Scholarship Details:

• Amount: $1,000 (minimum)

• Eligibility: Open to high school seniors enrolled at Whitefish High School who aspire to pursue
further education whether it be at a traditional 4-year, 2-year, technical school, or through
beginning work/apprenticeship.

• Application Requirements: Interested students must submit a TYPED application form along
with an essay addressing the provided prompt. Essays should be kept under 250 words (half
page).

• No Minimum GPA Requirement: We believe in the potential of every student and encourage
all eligible candidates to apply.

Essay Prompt

In 250 words (half-page) or less, respond to one of the following ideas that resonated with
Jack:

1. Can hard work beat talent?

2. The only purpose of life is to be truly alive. What does “to be alive” mean to you? Is it about
extreme activities and adrenaline, about love and feelings, or about achieving something
significant?

3. Jack converted to Judaism in 2010 and strongly believed in “Tikkun Olam” - “Repairing the
World” - which is the idea of actively working to make the world a better place through social
justice, charity, and positive actions. How will you do this in your life now and in your chosen
line of study?
