## **Intro:** The Flathead Democratic Women award scholarships yearly to encourage Flathead Valley students to reach their academic goals through higher education.

## **Rules:** The 2026 Scholarship application period will open in January of 2026.

### Apply for Scholarships

If you are interested in applying for a Flathead County Democratic Women Scholarship, please talk to your guidance counselor. We drop off applications to area schools in January. You may also use this form. The due date is April 3, 2026.
