##### Apply

NGPA members who wish to make a career in the aerospace industry or who work elsewhere and wish to increase the margin of safety and fun in General Aviation are invited to apply for the NGPA Scholarship Program. Explore how you can learn, prepare, and finally apply during our next application window. please read all the information available below before submitting an opportunity profile.

## View Our 2026 Scholarships

Please reference our Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) guide [here](https://www.ngpa.org/about_scholarships). Click [here](https://www.ngpa.org/applynow#pages) to apply.

The following scholarships are available in 2026:

| --- | --- | --- |
| **Scholarship** | **Amount** | **Description & Requirements** |
| **United Airlines/Aviate Scholarships**<br>**(Multiple Available)** | Variable | These scholarships, offered by United Airlines & Aviate Academy are for individuals currently in, or working to be in the United Aviate Academy program. Recipients will receive either a full ride Aviate scholarship, or a partial scholarship. Funds are only eligible to be used at United Aviate Academy. |
| **PSA Airlines Flight Scholarship** | $4,500 | This scholarship is for a person pursuing their CFI rating. |
| **Republic Airways Flight Training Scholarship** | $5,000 | This scholarship is for a person pursuing aviation flight training. This is a cash award. NGPA will send funds to the flight school where you are training. |
| **Republic Airways LIFT Academy Pilot Scholarship** | $5,000 | This scholarship is for a person who is a current or incoming LIFT student, and pursuing aviation flight training. This is a cash award. NGPA will send funds to the flight school where you are training. |
| **Republic Airways Maintenance Scholarship** | $5,000 | This scholarship is for a person pursuing a career as an A&P technician and current or incoming student for Part 147 program. |
| **Republic Airways Aviation Management Scholarship** | $5,000 | This scholarship is for a current student enrolled in aviation management program or related including dispatch certification program. |

**Universal Requirements:**

-Must be an active member of NGPA.

-Must be at least 18 years of age.

-Must have at least a current third class Medical (for flight training scholarships)
