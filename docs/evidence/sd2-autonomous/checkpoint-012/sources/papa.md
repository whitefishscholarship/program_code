# PAPA selected public program/FAQ evidence

### **SCHOLARSHIPS & GRANTS**

Pursuing a career in aviation can be a long and costly pursuit. At PAPA, we strive to do our part in helping ensure that no qualified individual who aspires to become an aviation professional has to give up on their dream, solely because of financial reasons.

Since 2021, we have awarded over **$3 million** in scholarships to PAPA members. We usually open up our scholarship window twice a year. Once in the spring and once in the fall. **Anyone** can apply as long as they are a PAPA member and meet the criteria outlined by the sponsoring partner. Most scholarships will be paid directly towards the recipient’s institution.

We are proud to provide annual scholarships in conjunction with our partner organizations. Here’s to breaking down barriers in aviation!

### **Frequently Asked Questions**

- #### When will I be able to see which scholarships are available?






The website will be updated for each Spring and Fall Scholarship window so check back around those times.

- #### Can non-PAPA members receive a scholarship?






No. You must be a member of PAPA to receive a scholarship award. To become a member, visit our [**Members Area**](https://www.asianpilots.org/member-scholarships).

- #### What are the scholarship requirements?






General requirements include being at least 18 years of age, high school diploma or GED equivalent, Each scholarship has its own independent requirements. Log in with your PAPA membership credentials to view each individual scholarship.

- #### I am not Asian, can I still apply for and receive a scholarship?






Yes! Everyone is eligible regardless of race and ethnicity. Some scholarships may be decided based on past or current involvement in the Asian community.

- #### How much is each scholarship worth?






Scholarships range anywhere from $2,500 to $7,500 generally. These are subject to change yearly depending on sponsorship.

- #### How long after the application deadline will we be notified if we are awarded a scholarship?






The application process takes our scholarship team about 2-3 weeks after the window closes to review and finalize our scholarship recipients.

- #### When do the scholarships open? How often are there scholarship opportunities?






The scholarship windows open in the Spring and Fall, providing two scholarship application opportunities.

- #### Can I apply for more than one scholarship?






Yes, you may apply to any scholarship that you are eligible for.

- #### Can I apply as an international student?






Yes. You must be able to work in the United States and be a green card holder.

- #### Can I apply for the United Aviate Academy scholarship as a current student?






Yes, you may apply as long as you have a conditional acceptance letter from the United Aviate Academy and be an active student at the application close date.

- #### How are scholarship funds disbursed?






Funds will be disbursed directly to your flight school/university via check.

- #### How soon do I need to begin utilizing my scholarship award and how quickly does it need to be used?






Your funds can be used as soon as it is awarded. It is to be used within one year of awarding.

- #### Can I be awarded a scholarship as a past recipient?






Yes, you can be awarded a scholarship as a past recipient.
