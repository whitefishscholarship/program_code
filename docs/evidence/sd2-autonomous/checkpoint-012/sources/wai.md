## The White Family Legacy Scholarship

## TWO OFFERED – each at $3,000 value

The White Family Legacy Scholarship offers two $3000 scholarships to individuals working on a fixed-wing aircraft instrument or multi-engine rating, or commercial or CFI certificate. Funds will be paid directly to your
flight school. Flight training must be completed within one year of receipt
of the scholarship.

Minimum requirements: private pilot certificate, 100 hours of flight
time, and a current copy of the written test results for the certificate/rating
sought. One letter of recommendation from a pilot who has flown with you.
The recipient will be chosen based on merit, career aspirations and dedication, and contributions to the community. In your essay, please address how
the funds will be used, and your professional goals. Also, please discuss any
community or volunteer service organizations you are a member of, including dates and hours of service and a summary of your activities.

**$1,000 value**

**$2,500 value**

## Women Military Aviators Dream of Flight Scholarship

## TWO OFFERED – at $3,000 each                 U.S. SCHOOLS ONLY

Women Military Aviators (WMA) seeks to preserve, for history, the
important role women have played in creating and supporting the American Spirit through their contributions to flight, aeronautics, and space.
These elite bands of women have not only a love of aviation but have put
their lives on the line in defense of their country through their military
service. WMA is committed to passing this legacy from one generation of
women to another and has established an annual scholarship program in
support of this commitment. Advocating the love of aviation, WMA offers an annual scholarship to advance a woman in her pursuit of flight ratings at an accredited institution or FAA Approved Part 141 or Part 61 flight
school. Over the past 30 years this scholarship has been given to select
candidates so that they may pursue opportunities in aviation.

Applicants must write about their demonstrated persistence and determination to flight.

State ability to complete training at an accredited institution or FAA
Part 141 or Part 61 flight school within one year of scholarship award.
Identify the school, college, university, Part 141 or Part 61 flight school
where training will be completed. Specifically address the program and/or
ratings being sought. Address your interest and/or participation in military
aviation. Address your financial need.
