# WMA selected program evidence

### What is the Dream of Flight Scholarship?

##### Women Military Aviators (WMA) seeks to preserve, for history, the important role women have played in creating and supporting the American spirit through their contributions to flight, aeronautics and space.

This elite band of women has not only a love of aviation but has put their lives on the line in defense of their country through their military service. WMA is committed to passing this legacy from one generation of women to another and has established an annual scholarship program in support of this commitment. Advocating the love of aviation,

WMA offers an annual scholarship(s) to advance a woman in her pursuit of flight ratings at an accredited institution or FAA approved Part 141 or Part 61 flight school. Over the past 30 years, the scholarship(s) has been given to select candidates so that they may pursue opportunities in aviation.

Each year, the Dream of Flight scholarship(s) is given in recognition of a member of our military community to honor their service or support of women military aviators.

##### Each applicant should supply - in addition to basic WAI requirements - a narrative addressing the following criteria:

- Demonstrated persistence and determination to fly.
- Stated ability to complete training at an accredited institution or FAA Part 141 approved school within one year of scholarship award.
- Identify the school, college, university, and/or Part 141/61 flight school where training will be completed, and address the program and/or ratings being sought.
- Address interest and/or participation in military aviation.
- Address financial need.

##### Text Applications are submitted through the Women in Aviation International (WAI) scholarship program.
