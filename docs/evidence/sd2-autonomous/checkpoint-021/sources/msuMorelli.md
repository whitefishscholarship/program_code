# Information: Hilleman / Morelli Scholarship

MSU is honored to continue the remarkable efforts of Joseph James Legacy Foundation
by awarding the Hilleman/Morelli Scholarship to Montana resident students. This 4-year
scholarship provides a unique blend of financial, academic, leadership, and mentoring
support for students with learning differences.  Hilleman/Morelli scholars will be
selected on their academic, leadership, and career potential.  We seek scholars who
are intrinsically motivated to impact their community with a degree in a science,
technology, engineering or math discipline

If you are ready to roll up your sleeves and get to work, know we are too! To apply
or learn more about this Hilleman/Morelli scholarship, please [visit the Hilleman Scholars Application page for more information](https://www.montana.edu/hillemanscholars/apply.html)
