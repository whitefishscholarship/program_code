# Submit an Application

This application is open for students starting at MSU in Fall, 2026!

Scholarship selection is accomplished by a group of dedicated campus partners who
believe in the importance of MSU’s land grant mission. The selection committee are
your partners and champions at MSU, help them make their difficult decision by giving
them a sense of your great potential to lead and your desire to earn a college degree.
Please complete this application with those details in mind. The selection committee
appreciates your honesty, humor, and your aspirations for the future. You are encouraged
to share your application with your guidance counselor, teacher, or coach for review
before submission. The committee is rooting for you! We are excited to receive your
application and learn more about you.

**Please submit the following information for a personal reference.** This may be a high school teacher, coach, counselor, or another significant influence (but not a family member).

Upon submission of your application, a representative from the MSU Hilleman Scholars Program will contact your reference to provide further instruction on completing the recommendation

Essay Question

After reading [Dr. Hilleman's biography](https://www.montana.edu/hillemanscholars/biography.html), please write a brief essay (approximately 500-1000 words) describing how his story inspires your own studies at Montana State University. Please also describe your personal aspirations and how you will channel your work ethic to achieve your academic, leadership, and/or career goals.
