## Criteria

#### Required Criteria

The following are required criteria for this scholarship. Applicants _**must**_:

- be enrolled in a two-year technical program at an accredited postsecondary institution by the start of the first term of the awarded period.

#### Preferred Criteria

Preference in consideration will be given to applicants who are:

- are from Blaine, Hill, or Phillips Counties in Montana.

#### Continuing Criteria

Scholarship recipients will be eligible to receive the awarded scholarship for 2 academic years. Current recipients of this scholarship looking to continue the scholarship for another year _**must**_:

- continue to meet the required criteria for this scholarship;
- remaining in good academic standing; _**and**_
- complete the MCF Continued Scholarship Form (sent via email in early Spring to each recipient).

#### Reapplication

Previous recipients are NOT allowed to apply again.
