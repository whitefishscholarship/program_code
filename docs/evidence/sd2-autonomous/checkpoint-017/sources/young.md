## Criteria

#### Required Criteria

The following are required criteria for this scholarship. Applicants _**must**_:

- be a high school senior graduating from a Montana High School.
- demonstrate a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher.
- be going on to college to study STEM (science, technology, engineering, mathematics) or healthcare.
- be enrolled, per the requirements of this scholarship, at an accredited postsecondary institution by the start of the first term of the awarded period.

#### Preferred Criteria

Preference in consideration will be given to applicants who are:

- plan to attend a college or university in Montana.

Selection is based on financial need, academic achievement, leadership ability, school and community participation, and personal attributes.

#### Reapplication

Previous recipients are NOT allowed to apply again.
