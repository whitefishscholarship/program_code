## Criteria

#### Required Criteria

The following are required criteria for this scholarship. Applicants _**must**_:

- be a graduating senior enrolled in the pre-nursing program at Montana Tech, be a current pre-nursing student at Montana Tech, _**or**_ be a nursing student in the Montana Tech Nursing Program.
- demonstrate financial need.
- demonstrate a minimum pre-nursing (high school) GPA of 2.0 or higher (or equivalent).
- demonstrate a strong commitment to community health.

#### Preferred Criteria

Preference in consideration will be given to applicants who are:

- are Butte-Silver Bow high school graduates.
- are nontraditional students. Strong preference will be given for students who possess one or more of the following characteristics: older than typical age, attending college part-time, independent of their parents, working full-time while attending college, have dependents, are a single parent, or who have achieved high school equivalency (HiSET or GED credential).

#### Reapplication

Previous recipients may apply again.
