## Criteria

#### Required Criteria

The following are required criteria for this scholarship. Applicants _**must**_:

- be currently employed or have a parent or legal guardian employed by Glacier Bancorp. (Children and step-children of employees are eligible, as are full or part-time students.)
- be enrolled, per the requirements of this scholarship, at an accredited postsecondary institution by the start of the first term of the awarded period.

#### Preferred Criteria

Geographic diversity of where employees branch is located is highly desirable and will be considered.

#### Reapplication

Previous recipients may apply again. Past scholarship awards do not disqualify students from future opportunities. After receiving an initial award, students can apply for additional scholarships, regardless of personal or parental employment status at Glacier Bancorp.
