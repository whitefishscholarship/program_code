### **ASE Scholarship**

In partnership with the National Institute for Automotive Service Excellence (ASE), NTHS is proud to offer a $500 scholarship to members enrolled in automotive, collision, or diesel coursework. Only NTHS members who have added the ASE Endorsement to their membership will be automatically entered into the scholarship drawing.

**Available: May 1 - April 30 \| Number Awarded: 1 \| Amount: $500**
