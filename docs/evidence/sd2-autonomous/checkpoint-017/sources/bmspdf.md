**Scholarship Application Instructions**

| Eligibility: | Applicants must graduate from a Montana High School or certified home school in 2024 and be continuing their education at an accredited institution of higher learning. Accredited institutions include trade schools, technical schools, colleges, or universities. |
| --- | --- |
| To Be Considered: | Consideration for the scholarship requires submission of a completed and signed application form along with your essay. |
| Essay Question: | How will you make a difference in our world? |
| Essay Instructions: | 1. Begin by stating the essay question followed by your essay. 2. Limit your essay to 500 words. 3. Use 11 pt. or 12 pt. size font. 4. Your essay needs to be double-spaced on a standard 81/2 × 11 inch page format. 5. Center page numbers at the bottom of each page. Do not include: Graphics, charts, pictures, school transcripts, letters of recommendation or other types of documents not specifically requested. These items are not evaluated and are disposed of if included. |
| Application Form: | The application form must be filled out completely. Exception: if the word “Undecided” is entered for the “Name of Institution” in Part III - Section B, the rest of the Section B fields may be left blank. Enter your information directly into the form. Be sure to proof your entry and save your file. Changes to your form will only be saved if you use the save command on the form tool bar! |
| Proof of Enrollment: | If a Letter of Acceptance or other Proof of Enrollment is not available when completing this application, the winning applicant must forward it to Black Mountain Software prior to the presentation of the scholarship. |
| Submitting the Application: | Your application and essay must be submitted electronically. Please email to: [contact redacted] The application must be emailed on or before April 15, 2023. |
| Scholarship Award: | Two scholarships will be awarded in the amount of $1,000.00. Each payment will be made directly to the school the applicant will be attending. |
