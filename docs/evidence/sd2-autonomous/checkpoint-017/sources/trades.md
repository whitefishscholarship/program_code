## Monthly Student Scholarships

Bring Back the Trades Inc. awards scholarships to students from across the United States who are attending post-secondary education in the Skilled Trades.

Monthly scholarships of **$1,500** are awarded and **_sent directly to the school_** the award recipient attends. Additional scholarships are awarded periodically as additional funds are raised through donations or sponsorships of our events. Students must have debt owed to the school to be eligible to win.

Applying is easy! Simply complete the [**Bring Back the Trades Student Scholarship Application Form**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScoV7uZcIHjWTZ0025J_fQKaTeK5g8Dx_Lo7gfq4FMGXhRE3g/viewform?usp=header) explaining why you deserve the scholarship.
