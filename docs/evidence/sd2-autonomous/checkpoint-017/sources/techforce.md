### **NTHS TechForce Scholarship**

[image excluded]

As part of our partnership, NTHS is providing **two $2,500 Scholarships** annually through the TechForce Platform. These scholarships are available to NTHS members pursuing careers in the skilled trades.

### **How to Apply:**

1. **Join the TechForce Network:**
   - To be eligible, students must create a TechForce account and join the network.
2. **Apply Through the Network:**
   - Once your account is active, complete the TechForce scholarship application in the TechForce platform and note that you are an NTHS member.

Don’t miss this opportunity to gain financial support for your education and career goals in the skilled trades!
