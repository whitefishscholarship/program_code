# Dell Scholars

The Dell Scholars program helps you earn your degree and launch your career. Each year, 500 students become Dell Scholars, gaining financial assistance, academic advising, career coaching, and a team of dedicated experts who support them every step of the way.


## A Program Built for You

We know that every student’s college experience is different. That’s why our program takes a personalized approach.



### Academic

Success in college starts with people who’ve got your back. Regular check-ins with advisors and resources help you stay on track—balancing classes, setting goals, and making big decisions.



### Financial

College is expensive, but we’re here to help. A $20,000 flexible scholarship covers tuition and housing—plus a laptop, book credits, and emergency funds—so you can focus on learning, not expenses.



### Professional

We provide guidance and resources to explore careers and build connections, helping you navigate options and prepare for your first job after college.



### Wellness

Your well-being matters. Dell Scholars get free teletherapy, helping you stay strong through college and into your career.







## Who Can Apply?

To be eligible for the 2027 application cycle, you must:

- Be a high school senior graduating from a Texas high school

- Participate in an approved [college readiness program](https://www.dellscholars.org/students/college-readiness-programs/) in grades 11 and 12

- Plan to enroll in an accredited four-year public college or university in Texas

- Be Pell Grant eligible

- Have at least a 2.4 GPA


Strong candidates demonstrate:

- A strong sense of purpose — setting clear goals and taking steps to achieve them.

- A career-driven mindset — seeing their bachelor’s degree as a stepping stone to a fulfilling career.

- Perseverance — turning obstacles into opportunities and finding ways to move forward.

- Self-motivation and determination – taking initiative, seeking opportunities, and continuing to push forward.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to often asked questions about the Dell Scholars program.

What is the Dell Scholars Program?

The Dell Scholars program is a scholarship and college completion program that nurtures and empowers students on their path to a college degree. Since starting the program in 2004, the Michael & Susan Dell Foundation has supported 6,000 scholars, including more than 4,500 college graduates.

How do I apply?

To apply for a scholarship from the Dell Scholars program, the first step is completing the online application. See application dates [here](https://www.dellscholars.org/students/#important-dates). Once submitted, all applications will be assessed, and a group of finalists will be selected. Finalists are asked to submit a current copy of their high school transcript, a FAFSA Submission Summary (FSS), and other required information.

How can scholarship funds be used?

Our scholarship is designed to be flexible so that scholars can use award money for the many costs that arise during their college career. In addition to funding tuition, fees, and full cost of attendance for undergraduate study, scholarship awards may be used towards graduate school, loan repayment, study abroad, and internship stipends.

How do I log in to Dell Scholars Connect?

Dell Scholars Connect is a private website for Dell Scholars to stay in contact with each other as well as with the foundation. Only those who have been awarded the scholarship will have access to this site. Log in [here](https://connect.dellscholars.org/login).
