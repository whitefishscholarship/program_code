# Fly for Duke Aviation Scholarship Criteria

Unique to the Fly for Duke Aviation Scholarships, each scholarship recipient will receive financial assistance and mentoring will be available upon request.

**Applicant Criteria**

- Must be between the ages of 17 – 24 years
- Must train at an FAA approved flight school
- Must complete private pilot ground school prior to commencement of flight training
- Must qualify for and provide a copy of an FAA 3rd class medical prior to flight training
- Must be a U.S. citizen

_Please note: The deadline for all applications is February 25, 2026 at 5:00 p.m. CST._
