# Fly for Duke Aviation Scholarships

The Duke Carrillo Foundation offers Fly For Duke aviation scholarships to aspiring aviators who are between the ages of 17 and 24 to earn a private pilot’s license. In addition to financial assistance, the scholarship includes mentoring upon request to support and guide the recipients as they go through training.

**Live Like Duke and Fly For Duke!**

Applications for the 2025 scholarship are now closed. Scholarship recipients will be notified by March 29, 2026 at the phone number and/or email you provided on your application.
