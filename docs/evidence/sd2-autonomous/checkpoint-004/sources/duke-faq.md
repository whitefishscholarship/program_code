# Fly For Duke Aviation Scholarship – Frequently Asked Questions

_Do I need to be a citizen of the United States to apply for the Fly for Duke scholarship?_

Yes. If selected for a Duke Carrillo Foundation Fly For Duke scholarship, proof of United States citizenship will be required. Examples are a current United States passport or certified copy of United States birth certificate. A valid photo ID will also be required.

_What are the age requirements?_

Currently the age requirements are 17-24 years of age at time of application for the Fly for Duke scholarship.

_Is there a deadline by which I must complete my private pilot’s license?_

Yes. Training must be completed by May 1, 2027.

_When will I be assigned a mentor?_

A mentor from the Duke Carrillo Foundation may be assigned to you upon request immediately after notification of receiving the Fly for Duke scholarship.

<PRIVATE_PERSON> I use the Fly for Duke scholarship for online training, in person training, and flight training?_

Yes. As long as all training is at an approved FAA flight school for private pilot’s license, all forms of training with that school will be accepted.

_When can I apply for the Fly for Duke scholarship?_

The Fly for Duke scholarship application opens on November 16, 2025 and closes February 25, 2026 at 5:00 PM CST.

_How do I apply for the Fly for Duke scholarship?_

The Fly for Duke scholarship will be available online starting November 16, 2025.

_If I am currently enrolled in a college or university, do you still need at high school transcript?_

No, please provide your college transcript.

_How and when will I be notified if I am chosen for a Fly for Duke scholarship?_

Scholarship recipients will be notified by March 29, 2026. Notifications will be sent by provided email.

_May I mail a completed Fly for Duke scholarship application to you?_

No. Only Fly for Duke scholarship applications completed online will be accepted.

_Are there any exceptions for late applications or recommendations?_

No late applications or recommendations will be accepted or reviewed.

_I was not awarded a Fly for Duke scholarship. May I reapply next year?_

Yes, you can reapply for a Fly for Duke scholarship when the next application period opens.
