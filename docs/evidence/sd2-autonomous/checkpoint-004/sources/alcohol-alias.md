## 3rd Annual Scholarship Winners Announced

**Congratulations to our 2026 scholarship recipients!** Five Montana high school seniors were selected from 64 applicants for their creative designs promoting alcohol responsibility. These original artworks will be featured on materials across the state to help keep our communities safe and informed.
