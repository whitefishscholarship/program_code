2025-2026 John & Joe Norrish Memorial Scholarship
                            Application Deadline—Friday, Feb. 27, 2026 4:00 PM

The John & Joe Norrish Memorial Scholarship program was created to honor and recognize FBA Member Builders John and
Joe Norrish, who were known for the high-quality homes they built and their commitment to the Flathead Valley community
and our industry. By supporting construction education, the John & Joe Norrish Memorial Scholarship program is committed
to encouraging students to strive to advance their career goals, help them gain the education necessary to achieve these
goals, develop more professionals in the construction industry, and help sustain the building industry as a valuable economic
sector in the Flathead Valley and the U.S.

PURPOSE:
To provide scholarship funds to eligible candidates as defined below who are planning to pursue an education in a construc-
tion related field.
DEFINITION OF TERMS:
“Construction related” field is defined as follows:
• Engineering, Construction Management, Architecture, Structural Design
• Carpentry, welding, electrical, heating, ventilation, interior design, landscape, plumbing, framer and building trades
• Business management, office management, financial accounting , banking, marketing
Note: If a student’s area of study is not listed above, evaluation of the student’s course of study and its relevance to the con-
struction industry will be at the Scholarship Committee’s discretion.

                                                             ELIGIBLITY:
Scholarship is applicable to any student pursuing these interests and will be evaluated based on the student
meeting relevant criteria in the following areas, according to their program of study:
•     Students in the community pursuing major coursework in a construction-related field of study or vocational training
•     If Graduating high school senior: GPA of 2.5 or higher
•     If Full-time undergraduate college student : GPA of 2.5 or higher with declared major
•     If Returning Adult Student: GPA of 2.5 or higher with at least one year of college entering established Certificate Program
      or formal course of study (declared major)
•     Major course of learning must be directly related to the construction industry or supporting field based on Student’s aca-
      demic plan and at the discretion of Scholarship Committee.
•     Students may be planning to pursue or currently enrolled in any accredited public/private community college, university
      or vocational/technical institution of higher learning in the state of Montana.
•     Weight given to children or legal dependent of FBA member or (2) Employee of FBA Member Company in good standing
      at time of application, or (3) Candidate recommended and sponsored by FBA Member in good standing at time of appli-
      cation (signature required on application if sponsored).
•     Weight given to local students planning to stay in or return to the Flathead Valley to live and work.
•     Weight given to first-time applicants/recipients versus repeat applicants/recipients.
•     Candidates meeting the application requirements listed but who have NOT yet declared a major are also encouraged to
      apply.

NUMBER, AMOUNT AND TYPE OF SCHOLARSHIPS AWARDED EACH YEAR:
• The number of scholarships awarded is at the discretion of the Scholarship Committee with final approval by FBA Board
  of Directors, based on available scholarship funds and the number of qualified applications.
• Amount of scholarship typically ranges from $500 to $1500, and is at the discretion of the Scholarship Committee with
  the approval of the Flathead Building Association Board of Directors
• To be used solely for tuition costs

TIMEFRAMES:
Monday, Dec. 15, 2025 - Scholarship application opens - packets issued to local High Schools & FVCC.
Friday, Feb. 27, 2026 - Deadline to apply, please submit by 4:00 PM.
April 2026 - Applications reviewed and student recipients selected by FBA Past Presidents Committee.
Student will be notified at in May/June 2026 at local scholarship award ceremonies or by email.

SELECTION CRITERIA:
Selection is based on a combined scoring and at the discretion of the Scholarship Committee with approval
from the FBA Board of Directors. Scholarships are based on qualifications including academics, personal
achievement, employment history and area of study.
FBA reserves the right to cancel scholarship funds at the discretion of the FBA Board of Directors should the
recipient be deemed ineligible at any time during the scholarship year.

SCHOLARSHIP AWARDS
Scholarships may be awarded at a school award ceremony. A press release may follow. FBA may request
awarded student attend an FBA event to receive recognition.
SCHOLARSHIP PAYOUT:
• Use of scholarship monies is restricted to tuition only.
• Funds can only be used for payment of academic year 12 months following selection and will not cover
   any academic year prior to selection
• A Letter of Acceptance and verification of enrollment from student’s educational institution must be
   received by FBA prior to any checks being issued.
• Payment of scholarship monies is paid directly to school in the name of the student.
• Payment is typically made in payments of 50% for first term or semester and 50% at the start of the sec-
   ond term or semester provided the student is in good standing with the school with a GPA of 2.5 or above
   during the first quarter or semester. Payment schedule is at the discretion of the Scholarship Committee
• Lower grades will be considered and reviewed by Scholarship Committee for circumstances.
• Schools will be notified that any funds not used within 12 months of selection must be returned to FBA -
   Norrish Scholarship program
SCHOLARSHIP COMMITTEE:
The John & Joe Norrish Memorial Scholarship Committee shall be a sub-committee of the FBA Past
President’s Council (PPC) and shall consist of a minimum of 5 volunteer members of the PPC. The
Chairperson of the Scholarship Committee will be the FBA Past President for that school calendar year. If a
child or legal dependent of a PPC member applies, that PPC member will not participate on the Scholarship
Committee for the round of selection involving the PPC member’s child or legal dependent. Decisions of the
Scholarship Committee with approval of the FBA Board of Directors shall be final.
SCHOLARSHIP PROCESS:
• Applications will receive acknowledgment of receipt through email or mail.
• Applications will be reviewed for minimum requirements and passed to the Scholarship Committee.
• Established scoring system will be used to score each application.
• Scholarship Committee has the option to interview applicants.
• Applicants notified of status of their application (incomplete, not selected or selected) via email or mail.
Applicants may withdraw from selection at any time by sending an email or letter to the Scholarship
Committee.
