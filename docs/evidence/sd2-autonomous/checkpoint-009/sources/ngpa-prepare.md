## Prepare to Apply

Our 2026 Application is now open [here](https://www.ngpa.org/applynow). If you are not yet a member of NGPA, join now by clicking the link [here](https://www.ngpa.org/member_levels_benefits). Membership is required to successfully apply for the NGPA scholarship program.

**What will I need to Apply?**

Consider gathering the following documents for your application:

- Photo ID;

- FAA aviation/aerospace industry certificates or your nation’s equivalent;

- High school academic diploma, or your nation’s equivalent of secondary school academic records;

- Junior college, trade school, and/or college transcripts, as applicable;

- Other certificates of training

- _APPLICANTS COMPETING FOR FLIGHT TRAINING SCHOLARSHIPS:_
  - _FAA medical (or your nation’s equivalent);_

  - _Logbook records – covering at a minimum your last 50 hours of flight training (no penalty for providing more) .  If you have fewer than 50 hours, scan and include records of all your flight training._

  - _Logbook records – all checkride flights and comments from your DPE in your logbook_

Consider compiling and polishing the following items **NOW** for upload and/or insertion into your application once opened:

- One-page resume;

- Your employment history with dates (accurate to month/year);

- Your community service history (accurate to month/year);

- Your LGBTQ+ advocacy, volunteer, or “Q+” centered club membership and participation history

- Consider how you will **concisely** articulate:
  - Why you deserve an NGPA scholarship;

  - 1-, 2-, 3-, 4-, and 5-year aviation-related goals, and plans to achieve them;

  - Your unique financial need, and the steps you have taken to mitigate your financial need to achieve your dreams;

  - How your record of community service and/or club participation reflects NGPA’s mission
