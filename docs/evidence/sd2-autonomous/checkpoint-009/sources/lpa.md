## Our Commitment

The Latino Professionals in Aerospace (LPA) is committed to our community by providing opportunities in aviation. LPA has established key partnerships with both aviation and non-aviation related organizations and companies that allow us to make scholarships available to a diverse group of recipients. These scholarships help recipients further their careers in aviation related fields.

## General Eligibility

All applicants must meet these requirements. Some scholarships have additional criteria.

1

#### Active LPA Member

Must be an active LPA member (Student, Standard, or Lifetime tier).

2

#### Age Requirement

Must be at least 17 years old. United Aviate Academy requires 18+.

3

#### U.S. Based Training

Must be enrolled or accepted to an accredited institution within the United States or its territories.

4

#### Direct to Institution

Scholarship amounts vary. All funds are sent directly to the certified educational institution.

## What You Need to Apply

Prepare these items before starting your application.

📄

#### Current Resume

One-page limit. Education, work history, volunteer experience, and honors.

👥

#### Two Recommendations

From professors, employers, or mentors. One page each. LPA members preferred.

✎️

#### Two Essays

500-word limit each. Showcase your passion and alignment with LPA's mission.

✅

#### Enrollment Verification

Proof of enrollment or acceptance. Required upon awarding of scholarship.

## Selected child programs

### LPA Aerospace Degree Advancement Scholarship

For students enrolled in or accepted into an accredited institution pursuing an aerospace field such as engineering, business, aircraft maintenance, air traffic control, dispatch or aerospace technology. Supports education, certifications and specialized training. Flight training is excluded.

Accredited InstitutionAerospace StudiesNo Flight Training

Award VariesAvailable awards: 3

### LPA Aviation Maintenance Technician Scholarship

For individuals pursuing careers as FAA certified Airframe and Powerplant mechanics and/or seeking Inspection Authorization. Supports tuition, technical training and required certifications. Applicants must be enrolled in or planning to enroll in an FAA recognized A&P program in the United States or its territories.

A&P / IA CertificationFAA Recognized ProgramU.S. or Territories

Award VariesAvailable awards: 4

### LPA Professional Flight Degree Scholarship

For students enrolled in or accepted into an accredited professional flight degree program. Supports tuition, flight fees and required training associated with university or collegiate aviation programs, helping students advance toward careers as professional pilots.

Accredited InstitutionProfessional Flight Degree

Award VariesAvailable awards: 2

### United Aviate Academy Scholarship

For aspiring professional pilots conditionally accepted to or currently enrolled at United Aviate Academy. Helps offset flight training costs so recipients can focus on their education and career preparation. Applicants must be at least 18 years old. Funds are paid directly to the Academy and cannot be transferred.

Age 18+UAA Accepted / EnrolledNontransferable Funds

Award $27,000–$122,000; amounts varyAvailable awards: 5
