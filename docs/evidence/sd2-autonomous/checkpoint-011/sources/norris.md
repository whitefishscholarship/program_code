ISA+21 awards two scholarships each year to aspiring female airline pilots.

**Prerequisite Information** Applicants must satisfy all of the following requirements: Hold an FAA Commercial Pilots License or equivalent Hold a command instrument rating In pursuit of a career as an airline pilot Hold a current first class medical certificate Conditions: Only one <PRIVATE_PERSON> Memorial Scholarship Award is allowed in a lifetime Applicants may apply for both the <PRIVATE_PERSON> Memorial Scholarship Award & ISA+21 Financial Scholarship in a given year Applicants may apply as many times as they wish Training will be conducted at US Aviation Academy or approved flight school Funds will be paid directly to the approved training organization The winner is responsible for travel, housing and personal expenses during training Open to female applicants, as indicated on their pilot certificate Process: Please complete this application form All applications will be assessed by the Scholarship Committee for eligibility Scholarship entries are due by **November 1st** each year The scholarship winner is announced in March of each year

### Please include the following information when submitting your application

1. A descriptive 500 word essay tell us about yourself, your achievements and explain your aspirations to become an airline pilot
2. Professional resume
3. Copy of all Pilot Licenses
4. Copy of your Aviation Medical Certificate
5. Copy of the last three pages of your log book
6. One letter of recommendation from a pilot who has flown with you
7. One personal letter of recommendation
8. Transcript for your highest level of academic study ( *if currently enrolled in a college/university)*
9. List of all FAA (or equivalent agency) check rides and their outcomes.
10. Details pertinent to letters of inquiry from FAA (or equivalent agency) regarding any aircraft incidents. Or select None
