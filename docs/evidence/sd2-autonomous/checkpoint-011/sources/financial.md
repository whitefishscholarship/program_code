### ISA+21 awards a minimum of two Scholarships of $5000 each year to aspiring

### female airline pilots, to be used towards an advanced flight training program.

#### Prerequisite Information

Applicants must satisfy all of the following requirements: Currently be enrolled in an advanced flight training program; or Have completed ab-initio flight training In pursuit of a career as an airline pilot Hold a current first class aviation medical certificate Conditions: Only one Financial Scholarship Award is allowed in a lifetime Applicants may apply as many times as they wish Scholarship funds to be used towards an advanced flight training program Funds will be paid directly to the approved flight training organisation The winner is responsible for travel, housing and personal expenses during training Training must be completed within the following twelve months Open to female applicants, as indicated on their pilot certificate Process: Please complete this application form All applications will be assessed by the Scholarship Committee for eligibility Scholarship entries are due by **November 1st** each year The scholarship winners are announced in March of each year

### Please include the following information when submitting your application

1. A descriptive 500 word essay tell us about yourself, your achievements and explain your aspirations to become an airline pilot
2. Professional resume
3. Copy of all Pilot Licenses
4. Copy of your Aviation Medical Certificate
5. Copy of the last three pages of your log book
6. One letter of recommendation from a pilot who has flown with you
7. One personal letter of recommendation
8. Transcript for your highest level of academic study ( *if currently enrolled in a college/university)*
9. List of all FAA (or equivalent agency) check rides and their outcomes.
10. Details pertinent to letters of inquiry from FAA (or equivalent agency) regarding any aircraft incidents. Or select None
