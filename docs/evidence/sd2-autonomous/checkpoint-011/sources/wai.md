## APPLICATION REMINDERS

• Be a WAI member by August 30, 2026, and with a membership expiration of March 30, 2027, or later.

• Apply for up to three scholarships.

• Scholarships are awarded at the 38th Annual Women in
Aviation International Conference in Savannah, Georgia, at
the Savannah Convention Center on Friday, February 26, 2027.

• Submit applications online by the September 1, 2026, 11:59
p.m. EST deadline.

• Most recipients of the WAI2027 scholarships will be notified
by December 15, 2026.

The requirements listed below are the minimum for all
scholarships unless otherwise indicated. Some scholarships
may require additional information. Please read each description thoroughly to make sure you meet all requirements.

**ALL APPLICATIONS SHOULD INCLUDE:**

• two (one-page) recommendation letters

• typed, descriptive 500-word essay

• professional resume

• copies of all aviation and medical certificates and the last three pages of your pilot logbook (if applying for flight training scholarships)

• other applicable information as requested for each specific
award/grant scholarship

• official WAI application form

• WAI online application portal submission

## Dare to Dream Scholarship

## THREE OFFERED – each at $4,000 value

A $4,000 scholarship will be awarded to a WAI member who is working toward an instrument or multi-engine rating, commercial, or CFI certificate.
Funds will be paid directly to your flight school, and training must be completed within one year. The applicant must have a private pilot certificate,
and will be chosen based on merit, career aspirations, and dedication, as well
as contributions to her community. One letter of recommendation must be
from a pilot who has flown with you. FAA Class 1-3 medicals will still be required and preferred; BasicMed will be considered. One or the other is required. Prerequisite—private pilot certificate.

## ISA+21 Financial Scholarship 2027

## TWO OFFERED – each at $5,000 value

The International Society of Women Airline Pilots (ISA+21) will award a
minimum of two scholarships valued at $5,000 each to women working
to acquire a CFI, CFII, or MEI certificate. Funds will be paid directly to an
accredited college or university with an aviation curriculum, an aviation
technical school, or a government-approved flight school. Training must
be completed within one year of the award.

## PSA Airlines Engineering Scholarship $3,000 value

PSA Airlines is proud to offer a $3,000 scholarship to a Women in Aviation International (WAI) member who is pursuing a career in aerospace engineering or a related aviation engineering field. This scholarship is designed to
support individuals who are working toward becoming leaders in the aerospace sector, with a focus on innovation, safety, and the future of flight.

## Boeing Maintenance Training Scholarship $7,500 value

Boeing’s commitment to general aviation includes a maintenance training scholarship for a female individual currently enrolled in an accredited
maintenance program, or an accredited college or university should have a
GPA of 3.0 or better (on a 4.0 scale).  The recipient will be chosen based on
passion for aviation, commitment to pursuing a career in aviation maintenance, and the ability to complete training and must include two reference
letters highlighting the applicant’s character qualities. Funds will be paid
directly to the school, and the training is to be completed within one year.

## Epic Flight Academy Aviation Maintenance Technician Scholarship

## TWO OFFERED – each at $25,000 value

Epic Flight Academy offers two scholarships for $25,000 each towards their
FAA Part 147 aviation maintenance technician program. This scholarship
supports entry level applicants to become an FAA-certified airframe and
powerplant (A&P) aviation mechanic.

Applicants may select one of Epic’s two aircraft mechanic campuses.
One is located in New Smyrna Beach, Florida. The other is located in Erlanger,
Kentucky at the Cincinnati/Northern Kentucky International Airport (CVG).

## GlennCo Electrical Repair Training Scholarship

## TWO OFFERED – each at $2,500 value

GlennCo Training offers two scholarships for a three-day Level 1 Aircraft Electrical Repair & Troubleshooting Class. This class provides entry level technician with the knowledge and comprehension to comply
with the standard wiring practices and procedures, as well as the competence and confidence to perform the skills required in troubleshooting of wiring faults to the ultimate goal of the repair as required in the
line maintenance environment.

This three-day curriculum is specifically targeted to provide the Line &
Base Maintenance Technician with the troubleshooting skills that strengthen on-time performance through instructor led and practical training. Students are provided with onsite tools and materials necessary to complete
individual project work. Visit www.GlennCoTraining.net for more details.
