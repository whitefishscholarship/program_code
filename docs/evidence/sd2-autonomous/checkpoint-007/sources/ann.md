Ann Marie Guenther Memorial Scholarship: Information Sheet

Scholarship Details:

Amount: $5,000 for a 2-year course; up to $10,000 for a 4-year course.
Eligibility:
This scholarship is available to graduating seniors of Whitefish High School or Whitefish Independent High School.
Students must be planning on attending an accredited 2-4 year post-secondary institution full-time (12 credit minimum per semester), including in-state or out of state.
There is no minimum GPA requirement for this scholarship.

Application requirements: (typed application using form below)
One letter of recommendation from an adult who knows them well and who is not related to the applicant
Official high school transcript
Description of applicant’s post-graduate plans (including institution, major, career, etc.)
Personal essay for the following two prompts (approximately 250-500 words each, more information in application form):
Please share the impact education has had on your life
What doors will an advanced education open for you?
