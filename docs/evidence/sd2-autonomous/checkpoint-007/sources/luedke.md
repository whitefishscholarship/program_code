The Luedke Family Scholarship

This scholarship is awarded by the Luedke Family Endowment and is intended to target students who have experienced hardships, challenges due to disability, or discrimination in some way and who understand the importance of education in improving their lives.

Scholarship Details:

Amount: $1,500 (renewable for up to 4 years)
Eligibility:
This scholarship is available to graduating seniors of Whitefish High School or Whitefish Independent High School who have experienced hardships, challenges due to disability, or discrimination in some way including but not limited to:  economically (low-income), marginalized racially/ethnically, etc.
Student must be pursuing further education at either a traditional 4-year college, 2-year community college, or technical/vocational school.
Student must enroll in the Fall immediately following High School graduation.
Application Requirements: Interested students must submit a TYPED application form along with essays addressing the provided prompts.  Alternately, someone in the community (neighbor, teacher, counselor, friend, etc.) may nominate a Senior year student at either Whitefish High School or Whitefish Independent High School by submitting a TYPED application form addressing the provided prompt.
No Minimum GPA Requirement: We believe in the potential of every student and encourage all eligible candidates to apply.

Student Essay Prompts
Please answer all three prompts below; each prompt should be a minimum of 250 words and approximately ½ page double spaced:
1. Personal circumstances and background:
Describe any unique challenges or barriers you have faced in your life that have impacted your pursuit of education and how you are working to (or have) overcome them.  (This could be anything from your current living situation and how it has affected your ability to focus on your studies, or family responsibilities, such as caring for siblings or parents, that affect your time and resources for school, or learning or cultural differences that have affected your situation, etc.)
2. Financial need:
Explain why you need financial assistance to pursue your educational goals.  What additional effort have you made to secure funding for your education?  (Such as working a job, applying for other scholarships or financial aid, etc.)
3. Future Impact:
How will you use your education for the benefit of others?
**OPTIONAL**
Are there any extenuating financial and/or additional personal circumstances or challenges you would like to share so we get to know more about you?

Community Nomination Essay Prompt
Please answer the prompt below; minimum 250 words, approximately ½ page double spaced:
Please describe why you believe this student is deserving of this scholarship.  In particular, describe the challenges or barriers the student has faced, how the student has handled those challenges or barriers, and how they have impacted the student’s pursuit of an education.
Yearly Renewal Criteria:
This scholarship is renewable and may run consecutively for up to 4 (four) years as long as the student:
Remains enrolled in an accredited 4-year college, 2-year community college, or technical/vocational school.
Submits a copy of their transcript (paper or electronic) each of the remaining years for which they are seeking a renewal scholarship to the Executive Director of the Whitefish Education Foundation, [mailing address omitted], or e-mailed to:  [email omitted].
Unless there are extenuating circumstances, the transcript must be submitted by 1 July in order for funding to be available for the Fall semester.  Failure to submit the transcript will result in the cancellation of the scholarship renewal.
The student must maintain a minimum GPA of “C” (2.0) to be eligible for the scholarship renewal.
The renewal amount will be the same as the original scholarship.
With 30-day notice to the student, the Whitefish Education Foundation and/or the Luedke Family Advisory Scholarship Selection Committee reserve the right to terminate the scholarship renewal for any reason the Foundation or Committee deems appropriate.
