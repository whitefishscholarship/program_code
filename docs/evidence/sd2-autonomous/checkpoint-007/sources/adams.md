Adams/Marcellin International Interest Scholarship
The Adams/Marcellin International Interest Scholarship, established by Greg Adams and
Colette Marcellin, is intended to help students who display an interest in global affairs, foreign
languages, or (a) culture(s) outside of their own lived experience.


Scholarship Details:


   ●​ Amount: $2,500 (one scholarship available each year)
   ●​ Eligibility:
       ●​ This scholarship is available to graduating seniors of Whitefish High School or
          Whitefish Independent High School.
       ●​ Student must be planning on attending an accredited 2-4 year post-secondary
          institution full time (12 credit minimum per semester), including in-state, out of
          state, or internationally
       ●​ Minimum GPA Requirement: A minimum GPA of 3.3 is required to be eligible for
          this scholarship
   ●​ Application requirements: (typed application using form below)
      ●​ Two letters of recommendation
      ●​ Official high school transcript
      ●​ List of Activities and Involvement
      ●​ Personal essay for the following two prompts (approximately 250 words each):
           o​ Describe how you developed an interest in a particular part of the world
              or global issues and how have you pursued that international interest? For
              example, how does it relate to your hobbies, studying a new language, types of
              volunteer work, travel choices, etc.
           o​ Describe how your future plans tie back to your international interests. For
              example, did it affect what you will study, what type of career you would pursue,
              etc.
