# Seeley Family Memorial Scholarship

## Eligible Student

Current College Students, Graduating High School Seniors

## Application Open

Thursday, January 01, 2026

## Deadline Date

Tuesday, March 31, 2026

## Background

<PRIVATE_PERSON> and <PRIVATE_PERSON>, members of the Helena First Presbyterian Church, created this scholarship to provide annual awards in perpetuity to benefit members of the church who are attending or planning to attend postsecondary school.

## Criteria

#### Required Criteria

The following are required criteria for this scholarship. Applicants _**must**_:

- be a member of the Helena First Presbyterian Church, an employee of Helena First Presbyterian Church, or be the a child of a member or employee.
- demonstrate a GPA of 2.0 or higher.
- be enrolled, per the requirements of this scholarship, at an accredited postsecondary institution by the start of the first term of the awarded period.

Selection for this scholarship is based on demonstrated financial need and good character.

#### Reapplication

Previous recipients are NOT allowed to apply again.
