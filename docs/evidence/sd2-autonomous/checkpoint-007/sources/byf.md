National Technical Honor Society


The [National Technical Honor Society](https://nths.org/scholarships-2/) annually awards five $2,000 scholarships to their members committed to pursuing careers in construction-related disciplines. Application requirements include:

- High School senior and/or post-secondary NTHS member
- Applicant personal information
- Listing of awards/honors/training participation/certifications received
- Letter of recommendation
- Three essay questions

Application Dates: October 1 – April 1
