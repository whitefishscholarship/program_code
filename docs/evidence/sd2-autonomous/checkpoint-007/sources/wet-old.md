# DIVERSITY SCHOLARSHIP APPLICATION FORM DUE DATE: APRIL 3, 2026

Water & Environmental Technologies, Inc. (WET) is a technical environmental and engineering consulting
firm committed to diversity, equity, and inclusion in the workplace. We believe innovation and creativity
come when companies advocate for a varied range of experience, talent, and ideas.

To continue our commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusion, we are offering a scholarship for first-year
college students who share our passion and vision to expand the perspectives shared and voices heard.
WET will award two (2) $1,000 scholarships.

This scholarship is open to all Montana and Wyoming High School students, but preference will be given to
those who are members of an underrepresented class*.

1. Print, or type the information on this form. Illegible or incomplete applications will not receive
consideration.

2. This application must be accompanied by a letter of recommendation. This letter should be
from a teacher, counselor, employer, or other non-family member that has knowledge of your
accomplishments.

3. This application must include an official transcript.

4. Attach to this application an essay about the following topic:

“What is the importance of diversity and inclusion?”

Please relate the topic to a personal experience (school project, summer employment, sports
activity, etc.) which was improved or made possible through diversity and/or inclusion.

a. Essay should be no more than 300 words.

b. Essay should be numbered and include the student’s name on each page.
