# 2026 SCHOLARSHIP APPLICATION

### Amount: $1,000.00

​Rhino Roofing believes in investing in the future of our communities. We are excited to award seven individual $1,000 scholarships to qualifying 2026 graduating high school seniors from Flathead, Lake, Missoula, or Ravalli Counties in Montana.

The recipients must have a minimum 3.0 GPA and will be notified the week of April 21, 2026. This scholarship may be used toward tuition, books, or expenses at any accredited college or trade school.
