### **Build Your Future Scholarship**

Through the support of the Build Your Future (an NCCER initiative), NTHS annually awards five $2000 scholarships to current NTHS members. To apply, you must be a current high school senior or post-secondary student studying a [construction-related discipline](https://media-upload-nths.s3.amazonaws.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/22141336/BYF-Codes.docx).

**Available: October 1 - April 1 \| Number Awarded: 5 \| Amount: $2,000**

[Apply](https://nths.org/byf-scholarship/)
