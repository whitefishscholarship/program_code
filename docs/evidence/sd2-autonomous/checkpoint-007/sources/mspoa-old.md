**MSPOA Merit Based Scholarship**

- Application will be available January 2027.

The Montana Sheriff's and Peace Officer Association awards two $1500.00 scholarships to be to students based on the following criteria and guidelines:

- Graduating high school senior who is seeking a career in local law enforcement OR a current college student who is pursuing a career in local law enforcement

**AND**

- Whose parent is a current MSPOA member in good standing AND is employed by a Sheriff's Office in the state of Montana.

Recipients will be selected on the required criteria above as well as on the basis of need, academic ability, and career goals.

Eligibility requirements are as follows:

1. Parent is a current MSPOA member in good standing AND is employed by a Sheriff's Office in the state of Montana.

2. High school graduate or successful GED completion.

3. Must be able to meet basic peace officer eligibility requirements as established by Montana statute and the Montana Peace Officers Standards and Training Council (MCA 7-32-303) (23.13.2-POST Certification).

4. Must have a high school grade point average of at least 2.5 and preferably plans to be enrolled as a full-time student in a Montana based college or university that offers a minimum AA degree in the Criminal Justice Field. OR must have a current college/university grade point average of at least 2.5 and be pursuing a degree in the Criminal Justice Field.

5. High School students applying for the fall scholarship must submit proof on enrollment intentions in a university or college. College Students must submit proof of enrollment at his/her current university or college.

6. When applying for a scholarship, the following documents are required:
   - Letter requesting consideration as an applicant.

   - Current High School transcript OR Current College/University Transcript.

   - Personal resume including references.

   - Outline of career goals.

   - Recent Photograph.

   - Statement of how you are currently financing your college education.

   - Letter from high school or college advisor verifying your academic abilities and attesting to the fact you are enrolled in or have applied for a criminal justice degree program.

The Scholarship award recipient(s) check for $1500.00 will be issued, in the applicant's name, and forwarded to the college/university in which he/she is enrolled.

**MSPOA Members' Children Scholarship**

■ Application will be available January 2027.

- Applicant must be a high school senior or a current college/university student.

- Parent must be a current MSPOA member in good standing AND be employed by a Sheriff's Office in the state of Montana.


Five (5) Scholarships of $1000 will be awarded to five separate members' children. The scholarship recipients will be randomly chosen and are not merit based. The scholarship funds are for higher education and will be sent directly to the university/college in which the recipient is enrolled.
