# Montana Coffee Traders Scholarship

The Montana Coffee Traders Scholarship Fund was established at Whitefish Community Foundation in 2020 to help MCT employees and community members with college expenses.

The Montana Coffee Traders Scholarship Fund was established at Whitefish Community Foundation in 2020. Two scholarships are awarded annually to college-bound students. One $1,000 scholarship will be awarded to an eligible MCT employee or to a child or legal dependent of a current MCT employee. The second $1,000 scholarship will be awarded to an eligible Flathead Valley resident. The Montana Coffee Traders Scholarship will accept applications from January 30 to March 31, 2026 at 11:59 p.m.  Successful high school candidates will accept this scholarship at their high school award ceremony.
