Dane Hunt Kindness Scholarship
Important Dates
Applications due by 4/1 to the WHS guidance counselor (WHS Guidance Office)

Anyone in the Whitefish Community can nominate a graduating Senior to receive this award.

Scholarship Details:
• Amount: $1,000
• Eligibility: Open to high school seniors enrolled at Whitefish High School who aspire to pursue further education whether it be at a traditional 4-year, 2-year, or technical school, through beginning work/apprenticeship, or traveling.
• Application Requirements: nominated via written statement by WHS staff, fellow students and/or people within the greater community, this WHS senior will have engaged in random acts of kindness.
• No Minimum GPA Requirement: We believe in the potential of every student and encourage all eligible candidates to apply.
