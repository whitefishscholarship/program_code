Helen Mulloy Student Scholarship Overview
Administered by the Whitefish Education Foundation
Important Dates
Typed Applications due by 4/3 to the WHS guidance counselor (WHS Guidance Office)
Scholarship Details:
Application format: typed.
Amount: $2,000 (# of awards vary by year) renewable scholarship (if student maintains a 3.5 average in school)
Minimum GPA: 3.5
Eligibility: Open to high aspires seniors who are US Citizens and enrolled at Whitefish High School who aspire to pursue further education whether it be at a traditional 4-year school.
Application Requirements:
Essay
You may choose from one you wrote for your college application OR choose from 1 of our 2 essay prompts.
Financial Statement from parent/guardian (see the WHS guidance counselor if you are unable to obtain)
This statement should illustrate what your need for financial assistance is.
Transcript
Must include 7th semester grades; a photocopy that includes the signature of school official is acceptable.
Counselor Report
Must be completed and signed by the Counselor.
ACT/SAT Test Scores
1 set of scores; if available on your transcript you do not need to include a separate copy **NOTE: SAT essay score not taken into consideration
Letter(s) of Recommendation (must be typed, dated and signed)
1 letter from a HS teacher (could highlight things such as: work habits, leadership, character, personal integrity, etc.), and
1 letter from a community member NOT affiliated with the HS, and NOT related to you (could highlight things such as: participation in the community for work, leadership skills, etc.)
Employment History
Jobs held and average hours per week.
Leadership & Extracurricular Examples
List HS roles and leadership activities, # of years involved and hours per week worked on average.
Honors & Awards
List any awards and honors that you may have received during HS
Community Service
List community service organizations and TOTAL hours served.
