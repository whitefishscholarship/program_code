# Giving Together for the Flathead Valley

## MONTANA COFFEE TRADERS SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM

**This program is managed by Whitefish Community Foundation and will award two, $1,000
scholarships in 2026:**

• **One, $1000 scholarship will be awarded to an eligible Montana Coffee Traders
employee, or to a child or legal dependent of a current employee.** The applicant must
have been employed by Montana Coffee Traders within the year prior to the date of the
application, or must be a child or legal dependent of a current employee of Montana
Coffee Traders.

• **One, $1000 scholarship will be awarded to an eligible Flathead Valley resident.**
Applicant is not required to be associated with Montana Coffee Traders, but this
scholarship may be awarded to an eligible employee or child of an eligible employee if
the scholarship committee deems the applicant to be the best candidate.

## SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM HISTORY:

Montana Coffee Traders (MCT) has been roasting coffee in their vintage farmhouse just south
of Whitefish since 1981. With a current team of over 115 people across four Flathead Valley
locations, MCT strives to be a positive part of people’s everyday lives. As the company
continues to grow and evolve, MCT works to deepen roots in the local community and further
develop a connection to coffee farmers around the world.

## APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS AND ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS:

**The application deadline is March 31, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. MDT. Late applications will not be
accepted.** Awards will be announced in May, 2026.

Applications must be submitted online. The online application is available during the application
window by logging into Whitefish Community Foundation’s scholarship portal at:
https://www.grantinterface.com/Home/Logon?urlkey=wcfscholarship.

Applicants must meet the following requirements to be eligible for scholarship consideration:

• Have a high school or current college G.P.A. of 3.0 or higher (High school or college
transcripts must be uploaded with the application.)

---

• Preference will be given to individuals with a proven history of community
involvement/volunteerism and a strong work ethic.

• Provide a letter of recommendation from a current or former
teacher/professor/instructor written in the academic year of the application **and** a letter
of recommendation from a current or former employer or community involvement
supervisor written in the academic year of the application. (NOTE:  **Letters of
recommendation will be uploaded by the person providing the reference, not by the
applicant. Reference providers must upload their letters by the application deadline.)**

• Three essays written by the applicant addressing the following topics:

o Special attribute(s) or accomplishment(s) that set the applicant apart from
the crowd

o What the applicant believes they are capable of achieving once they have
earned a college degree. This may be explained in terms of life goals, career
goals, and/or goals within the community.

o Description of volunteer activities in which the applicant has chosen to
participate, why applicant chose those particular volunteer activities, and
how those experiences have affected applicant’s life.

• Intend to enroll at a post-secondary educational institution in the fall of 2026 (all
scholarships are paid directly to the institution upon verification of enrollment).

• Submit a completed application by the application deadline.
