John Michaels Memorial Scholarship

Scholarship Details:

   • Amount: $2,000

   • Eligibility: Open to high school seniors enrolled at Whitefish High School who
       aspire to pursue further education in a 4-year, 2-year, or technical school
       within Montana.

   • Application Requirements: Interested students must submit the application form
       along with an essay addressing the provided prompt. Essays should be kept
       under 500 words.

   • No Minimum GPA Requirement: We believe in the potential of every student
       and encourage all eligible candidates to apply.
