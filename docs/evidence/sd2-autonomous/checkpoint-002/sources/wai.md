# WAI2025 Scholarships

*(As of October 9, 2024)*

ursue your dreams and apply for WAI2025 scholarships! On August 1, the WAI2025 scholarship cycle began with additional listings and application details online at www.WAI.org/scholarships. 
**P**

Start reviewing the opportunities to support interests in aerospace engineering, dispatch, flight training, and general career enhancement. Remember, you must be a WAI member by October 1, 2024, to apply for up to three scholarships. Applications are due October 11, 2024, 
11:59 p.m. EST. Most scholarship recipients will be notified by the end of 2024, and all will be celebrated during the 36th Annual Women in Aviation International Conference in Denver, Colorado, March 27–29, 2025.



### Alaska Airlines/Horizon Air Maintenance Scholarship

### ($5,000 value)

Alaska Airlines will award one $5,000 scholarship to a female mechanic 
pursuing an A&P certification with the goal of working as a mechanic for 
Alaska Air Group. Applicants must be students enrolled in an accredited 
aviation maintenance program or in Alaska Airlines Maintenance Technician Development program and in good academic standing.


### Bunny M. Connors Memorial Scholarship

### ($979.82 value)

This scholarship is in memory of Bunny M. Connors, a career respiratory therapist at several hospitals in Massachusetts, who later chose to 
pursue her lifelong dream of flying. She became a flight instructor and 
had a commercial airline career. The scholarship will be awarded to a WAI 
member pursuing a private pilot certificate or instrument rating. Flight 
training must be completed within one year of receiving the scholarship.


### Christine Reed Memorial Flight Scholarship

### ($2,500 value)

Women in Aviation International (WAI) in partnership with J. Elliot 
Marketing (Nipomo, CA), offer a

$2,500 flight training scholarship in memory of Christine Reed. Christine never completed her flight training. She loved airplanes and loved 
airports, and she attempted to become a private pilot. Christine passed 
away unexpectedly at the age of 32 and was never able to complete her 
flight training.

The Christine Reed Memorial Flight Scholarship is open to women 
ages 16-30 that have soloed within the last six months preceding applying for this scholarship and are working toward their recreational, sport 
pilot, or private pilot certificate – advanced certificates/ratings may be 
considered depending the quality of the applicant pool. Applicants must 
provide a copy of their solo endorsement with the application. Preference will be given to New Jersey, Illinois, and Arizona residents but all 
applicants will be considered. Fund will be paid directly to the flight 
school of your choice.

In addition to all of the WAI scholarship requirements (including the 
scholarship essay), please also submit a 1-2 page essay explaining “Why 
I Love to Fly.”


### Delta Air Lines Aircraft Maintenance Technology Scholarship

### ($5,000 value)

Delta Air Lines will award a $5,000 scholarship to a student currently enrolled in an Aviation Maintenance Technician Program (A&P) or pursuing 
a degree in Aviation Maintenance Technology. The scholarship includes 
a trip to the WAI2025 conference in Denver (includes roundtrip airfare, 
hotel accommodations and conference fee). Applicant must be a full-time 
student with a cumulative 3.0 GPA or better and must be a U.S. citizen or 
eligible non-citizen.


### Delta Air Lines Pilot Scholarship

### ($5,000 value)

Delta Air Lines will award a $5,000 scholarship to a WAI member currently 
working toward their instrument, commercial, CFI, CFII, MEI, and primary certificates. The scholarship recipient will receive a trip to the WAI2025 
conference in Denver (includes roundtrip airfare, hotel accommodations, 
and conference fee). Scholarship funds will be paid directly to your flight 
school and training must be completed within a year. This scholarship is 
intended for women seeking advancement in professional aviation development toward a career as a professional airline pilot.


### Delta Air Lines Propel Pilot Scholarship

### ($5,000 value)

Delta Air Lines will award two $5,000 scholarships to WAI members currently participating in Delta’s Propel Program – either collegiate, company, 
or community partner path. The scholarship recipient will receive a trip to 
the WAI2025 conference in Denver (includes roundtrip airfare, hotel accommodations, and conference fee). This scholarship is intended for women 
seeking advancement in professional aviation development toward a career 
as a professional airline pilot.


### Flexjet Aircraft Maintenance Scholarship

### ($2,000 value)

Flexjet will award one $2,000 scholarship to a student currently enrolled 
in an Aviation Maintenance Technician Program (A&P) or a degree in Aviation Maintenance Technology. Candidates will be evaluated on academic standing, personal accomplishments, teamwork, leadership skills and 
community service involvement. Requirements: Meet all WAI scholarship 
requirements and guidelines, GPA must be 3.0 or higher, enrolled in an 
accredited U.S. maintenance program, U.S. citizen or permanent resident. 
Flexjet’s Maintenance & Engineering departments are vital to the company’s commitment to provide safe, dependable and friendly air transportation to their owners. As such, we seek to attract top maintenance talent 
who share their passion and interest in the aviation industry. To complement the scholarship, Flexjet will host the winner at our maintenance facility in Cleveland, Ohio, along with a tour of Flexjet headquarters and a 
meet and greet with industry leaders.


### Level Up Aviation General Scholarship

### ($1,000 value)

## Scholarship 2025

Level Up Aviation is a non-profit 501(c)(3) that allocates resources and opportunities needed to reach equity for economically disadvantaged and underrepresented groups in aviation or related fields. Level Up Aviation General 
Scholarship provides $1,000 for general career advancement in an aviation 
related career (maintenance, ATC, scheduling, engineering, etc). Applicants 
must be enrolled in a certification or degree program.  This scholarship will 
be paid directly to the organization providing the training to the applicant.



### The Dorothy Thompson/Tampa Bay Aviation Scholarship

**($1,500 value)**

Tampa Bay Aviation is offering one scholarship to a WAI member pursuing a fixed-wing or rotorcraft, private or commercial pilot certificate, 
or instrument rating. The scholarship will be in the form of a credit on 
their account at Tampa Bay Aviation. Funds will not be disbursed directly to the recipient. The recipient will be responsible for all written 
and oral examination fees, as well as any other fees which do not consist 
of aircraft rental or instruction at Tampa Bay Aviation. The scholarship 
must be used in pursuit of a certificate or rating and cannot simply be 
used for aircraft rental.


### WAI Atlanta “Wheels Down South” Chapter Scholarship

### ($1,000 value)

The WAI Atlanta Chapter is offering a scholarship to aid an individual with 
the pursuit of their aviation dream. Applicants will be evaluated based on 
merit, career, aspirations, dedication, contributions to the community, and 
following application directions. Within the required 500-word essay include the details of your aviation dream (flight, maintenance, management, 
etc.), how this scholarship will help you reach your goal, where you hope 
to use the funds, and an approximate timeline of completion of your goal.

Preference will be given to members of the Atlanta Chapter, but all applicants will be considered based on the criteria above. Award will be paid 
directly to the organization, not to the scholarship recipient. Applicants 
must use funds within one year of acceptance.
