# How to Apply

#### General WAI2027 Scholarship Application Requirements

Applications for the 2027 general WAI scholarship program are open from June 1, 2026 - September 1, 2026. **Only current members of Women in Aviation International are eligible to apply.** You may apply for up to three scholarships. The deadline to submit an application for the general WAI2027 scholarships is September 1, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. ET.

Scholarship applications are only accepted through the WAI website via the online portal. Please do not mail or email hard copies of your scholarship application. **The applicant must be a current WAI member with an expiration date no later than March 30, 2027, or join as a new member by August 30, 2026.**

All applicants must submit the following:

- Two letters of recommendation. Letters should be no more than one page, and cannot be from family members.
- One 500-word essay focused on what inspired you to join the aviation or aerospace industry, what keeps you motivated in this field, your contributions to the community, and how this scholarship will change your life.
- Resume (no more than two pages). Do not include a photo of yourself on your resume.
- If applying for a flight training scholarship, please include copies of your pilot and medical certificates and the last three pages of your pilot logbook. This is not necessary for other types of scholarships.
- Other information, if requested for each specific award/grant/scholarship.
- All documents must be 5MB or smaller to be accepted in the portal.

These requirements are the minimum for all scholarships unless otherwise indicated.  Please read the description thoroughly to make sure you meet all requirements. Each scholarship may have unique requirements.

Your essay should include the following:

- Your name and contact information
- What inspired you to join the aviation or aerospace industry
- Your community involvement
- How a scholarship will help you achieve your goals
- Other information as requested for each specific scholarship

Resumes should include the following:

- Work history or school studies
- Any educational awards or honors
- Demonstrated involvement in aviation-related activities
- Volunteer history
- Certificates already obtained, date, and flight hours completed (only if applying for a flight training scholarship.)

All applicants must be current WAI members with an expiration date of March 30, 2027, or later, or join WAI by August 30, 2026.
