Eligibility criteria:
Must currently be enrolled in art or music classes
Must plan on majoring in Art or Music (2- or 4-year course)
GPA not a determining factor
Have experienced or are currently experiencing hardships that have made continuing education especially challenging, including (but not limited to):
Mental health struggles/PTSD
Drug or alcohol addiction
Chronic health issues
Financial problems
Unplanned pregnancy
Homelessness
Checks will be made out to educational institution.
To apply, please include:
Your full name, address, email, phone number, educational institution and major.
A short essay (a few paragraphs) on why you should be considered for this scholarship.
A small portfolio of your art and/or musical accomplishments.
Letter of recommendation from an adult who knows the student
