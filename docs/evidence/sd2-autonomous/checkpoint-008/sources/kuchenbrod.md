Michael Kuchenbrod Memorial Scholarship
Criteria for first year applicant:
Whitefish High School graduate
Minimum 2.5 GPA from high school
Attend a 2-yr, 4-yr, or vocational/trade post-secondary institution in Montana full time (12 credit minimum per semester)
Renewable criteria
Awardees from year 1 are eligible for renewal for up to 3 additional years
The student must contact the Kuchenbrod family in writing with any or all of the following information once per year:
Current major, GPA, courses that have sparked interest/been rewarding or challenging, college life experiences or updates
The student must contact Whitefish Education Foundation and provide the following:
Transcript from year 2-4 with evidence a 2.5 minimum cumulative GPA
Evidence of 12 credit minimum schedule for years 2-4
Application elements:
Personal essay approximately 500 words/one page.
Describe your plans for the future, including your educational, career, and personal goals.  What inspired your goals, and how do you plan to achieve them?  Explain how receiving this scholarship will support your journey and help you make a positive impact in your community and/or chosen field.  Include specific details about your intended major, career path, or areas of passion and interest.  Discuss any role models, experiences, or events that shaped your future goals.
At least one letter of recommendation (no more than 2) from any adult other than a relative that knows the applicant well.
One page resume
Official high school transcript
Calculated SAI from your FAFSA
Acceptance letter from a Montana post secondary institution
Submit all of the above to [school contact omitted] by the end of May 7th, 2026.
Amount and follow up action
One $5000 award each year to a new WHS graduate
Each recipient may renew (for $5000) each year up through their 4th year of post-secondary education
Checks for each award will be written to the institution in the student’s name after all documents (letter of acceptance and registration of classes) have been submitted to WEF.
