# THE RIPPLE EFFECT SCHOLARSHIP APPLICATION PROCESS

The scholarship is available to any Whitefish or Columbia Falls senior entering a two- or four-year college
program with plans to major in an environmental science field.  Appropriate courses of study for
consideration in the scholarship program include: biology, ecology, environmental science/studies,
hydrology, or other fields related to water quality as approved by WLI staff. To qualify, students must also
have a 3.0 minimum grade point average.

*Scholarship amount is $500*

## Scholarship Applicant Responsibilities

In addition to completing a Scholarship Application Form (attached below), students must submit:

-Transcripts

-A 300-500 word essay describing your “watershed ethic.” In short, what is your philosophy
regarding human activities that affect water quality? How does your watershed ethic make you a
good steward of water resources?

-Email and phone numbers of TWO teachers to be used for references.

## Deadlines

• Completed applications and attached documentation for the **College Scholarship** must be postmarked
by **April 15th** and delivered to the Whitefish Lake Institute to be considered.

All scholarship recipients will be required to complete and submit to WLI by September 1<sup>st</sup> a Scholarship
Recipient Form requiring your Social Security Number (for proper accounting and tax records), the full
name and mailing address for the college you’ll be attending, and the semester in which funds will be used.
Funds will be sent directly to the college.

## Important Dates

April 15<sup>th</sup> Completed College Scholarship Application with Required Materials Postmarked for delivery
to WLI

May 1<sup>st</sup> Scholarships awarded

September 1<sup>st</sup> Scholarship Recipient Form Postmarked for delivery to WLI (Student will receive form upon
being awarded scholarship).
