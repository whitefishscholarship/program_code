## Our Commitment

The Latino Professionals in Aerospace (LPA) is committed to our community by providing opportunities in aviation. LPA has established key partnerships with both aviation and non-aviation related organizations and companies that allow us to make scholarships available to a diverse group of recipients. These scholarships help recipients further their careers in aviation related fields.

## General Eligibility

All applicants must meet these requirements. Some scholarships have additional criteria.

1

#### Active LPA Member

Must be an active LPA member (Student, Standard, or Lifetime tier).

2

#### Age Requirement

Must be at least 17 years old. United Aviate Academy requires 18+.

3

#### U.S. Based Training

Must be enrolled or accepted to an accredited institution within the United States or its territories.

4

#### Direct to Institution

Scholarship amounts vary. All funds are sent directly to the certified educational institution.

## What You Need to Apply

Prepare these items before starting your application.

📄

#### Current Resume

One-page limit. Education, work history, volunteer experience, and honors.

👥

#### Two Recommendations

From professors, employers, or mentors. One page each. LPA members preferred.

✎️

#### Two Essays

500-word limit each. Showcase your passion and alignment with LPA's mission.

✅

#### Enrollment Verification

Proof of enrollment or acceptance. Required upon awarding of scholarship.

### LPA Dispatcher Certification Scholarship

For individuals pursuing FAA Aircraft Dispatcher certification. Supports tuition for an approved FAA Aircraft Dispatcher certification program. Applicants must be enrolled in or accepted into an accredited educational institution. Dispatchers work alongside pilots to plan routes, monitor weather and support safe, efficient flight operations.
