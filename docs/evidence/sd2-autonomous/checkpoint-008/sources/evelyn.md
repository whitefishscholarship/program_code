# <PRIVATE_PERSON> Memorial Scholarship

## Eligible Student

Graduating High School Seniors

## Application Open

Thursday, January 01, 2026

## Deadline Date

Thursday, April 30, 2026




#### Required Criteria

The following are required criteria for this scholarship. Applicants _**must**_:

- be a member of the Order of the Rainbow for Girls.
- be a graduating high school senior from a Montana high school.
- demonstrate a high school GPA of 2.0 or higher
- plan to be enrolled in a degree program in an accredited post-secondary educational institution (university, college, community college, college of technology, or technical school).

Selection is based on the following factors: financial need, participation in school, community, and church activities, long-term goals, and motivation.

#### Reapplication

Previous recipients are NOT allowed to apply again.

## Awards

This scholarship typically provides the following each academic year.

#### Award Type

#### Amount per Award

Typically **$3,000** per academic year.

Typically **1 to 2** recipients each year.
