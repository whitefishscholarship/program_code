WHITEFISH ROTARY SCHOLARSHIP
The Whitefish Rotary Club will award scholarships to graduating seniors from Whitefish High School.  The number and dollar amount of scholarships awarded will depend on the number of quality applications received and approved but will be a minimum of $1000 per scholarship.  Scholarships from Whitefish Rotary Club may be used for any higher education pursuit: 2yr programs, 4yr programs, trade and vocational schools.
To be considered, an applicant will need to meet the following criteria:
Attend Whitefish High School for the entirety of their senior year and graduate from Whitefish High School.  (Gap year applicants accepted).
Be accepted to and plan to attend a Vocational Tech Institution, Community College, College/University, or a Trade-School Program.  (Gap year applicants accepted but must provide confirmation of approval of gap year).
Attend the Whitefish High School Awards Ceremony – a representative from Whitefish Rotary will be present to award the scholarship.
Attend a Whitefish Rotary regular lunch meeting to formally be recognized by our club, tell our club where you plan to attend in your pursuit of higher education, the program or degree you are interested in studying, and accept the scholarship.
The scholarship will be paid in one installment of $1000 or two installments of $500 per semester.
The scholarship funds will be made payable to the institution or program you are registered for and sent to the institution by our Treasurer (so please provide the appropriate information prior to your first semester or when you receive the bill for tuition).  The funds are to be credited to the student’s account for payment of tuition or other applicable higher education expenses.  In the event the scholarship money provided by Whitefish Rotary is not immediately needed because of other scholarships or financial benefits received, the scholarship money will be held for up to 2 years by our club and it is your responsibility to notify Whitefish Rotary when the scholarship money is needed at that time.
