HVAC

The HVAC trades, including heating, ventilation, and air conditioning, are essential for the comfort and health of society.

Technically qualified and ethical professionals who uphold the highest standards in customer service, safety, and business integrity should be uplifted as they pursue their academic and professional goals. By investing in their development, we ensure a stronger, more sustainable future for the HVAC trade.

This scholarship aims to support students who are entering the HVAC field so they can afford tuition, materials, and other expenses.

Any undergraduate student pursuing an [HVAC](https://bold.org/scholarships/by-major/trade-school-scholarships/) program at Chattanooga State Community College in [Tennessee](https://bold.org/scholarships/by-state/tennessee-scholarships/) may apply for this scholarship opportunity.

This is a no-essay scholarship - to apply, simply hit "Apply". Although no essay is required, a complete and detailed student profile is preferred.
