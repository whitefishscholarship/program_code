HERRIGES FAMILY MEMORIAL SCHOLARSHIP
DEADLINE: APRIL 20, 2026
AMOUNT OF AWARD: $3000
NUMBER OF AWARDS: Three
ESTABLISHED: 1994
 The Herriges Family Memorial Scholarship was established in memory of
 Nicholas and Anastasia Herriges by their children, all of whom were educated
 in the Whitefish schools. They remain interested in the Whitefish school
 system.
EACH APPLICANT MUST:
 1. Complete a formal written application for the scholarship
 2. Include a high school transcript and senior resume with the completed application
 3. Resume should cover community service, extra curricular activities and employment

 4. Attend a school in the state of Montana
 5. Submit completed information to the guidance counselor
