2025 E.A. HINDERMAN MEMORIAL SCHOLARSHIP
**INCOMPLETE APPLICATIONS WILL NOT BE CONSIDERED**
Deadline: April 18th
Amount of Award: Undetermined
Number of Awards: Undetermined
EACH APPLICANT MUST:
1. Make a formal written application for the scholarship
2. Attach a 7th semester Whitefish High School Transcript and a Senior Resume to
your completed application. RESUME MUST CONTAIN PERSONAL CELL
PHONE AND EMAIL ADDRESS.
3. Attach TWO (2) LETTERS OF RECOMMENDATION
4. Have demonstrated a need for financial aid. Attach a copy of the EFC (Estimated
Family Contribution from FAFSA form)
5. Have made an application and been accepted to a MONTANA INSTITUTION OF
HIGHER LEARNING OR TRAINING. This includes colleges, universities, vo-tech, technical, trade school, cosmetology school, etc. Include a copy of acceptance
letter
6. Use scholarship during the same year as high school graduation. MUST BE IN
CONTINUOUS ENROLLMENT/ATTENDANCE, AS A FULL TIME
STUDENT, TO OBTAIN & MAINTAIN SCHOLARSHIP. Amount of award
may be dispersed over two (2) academic years, dependent upon institution.
7. Turn all application materials into the counselor by the announced deadline.
8. Keep the counselor informed in regards to changed in application status. (Need
for financial aid decreases, post secondary plans, etc.)
