## Scholarships for Prospective Students

The Davidson Honors College (DHC) offers merit-based scholarships to incoming students to recognize outstanding talent, academic performance, and leadership potential. These are the University of Montana's premier academic scholarships and include the Presidential Leadership Scholarship (PLS), Provost’s Honors Scholarship (PHS), and the Dean’s Leadership Scholarship (DLS).

These scholarships reduce the cost of attendance at the University of Montana and students who meet progression standards may have their awards renewed for up to four years.

We are looking for well-rounded, ambitious, and curious learners. While we look at academic performance and achievements, these criteria are not absolute and all highly motivated students are encouraged to apply.

Montana high school graduates should also apply for the [Montana University System Honors Scholarship](https://mus.edu/Prepare/Pay/Scholarships/MUS_Honor_Scholarship.html).

- [Step 2: Apply to DHC](https://beagriz.umontana.edu/portal/bear_tracks)

## Scholarship Details

- The Fine Print

Scholarships may be waivers or donor funds. Some of the largest donor awards may defray the cost of attendance beyond tuition. All scholarship funds will be awarded based on donor criteria and available awards.

  - Maximum PLS Scholarships waivers are offered using the following criteria:
    - [Non-resident student tuition](https://www.umt.edu/finaid/cost-of-attendance/bachelors-deg-COA/out-of-state-coa.php) may be waived to the [WUE tuition rate](https://www.umt.edu/finaid/cost-of-attendance/bachelors-deg-COA/wue-coa.php).
    - WUE tuition may be waived to the [in-state tuition rate](https://www.umt.edu/finaid/cost-of-attendance/bachelors-deg-COA/in-state-coa.php).
    - Montana residents may have tuition fully waived by [certain eligible waiversPDF (PDF document)](https://mus.edu/borpol/bor900/940-13.pdf).


      - All Montana residents wishing to be considered for DHC scholarships must apply for the [Montana University System Honor Scholarship](https://www.mus.edu/Prepare/Pay/Scholarships/MUS_Honor_Scholarship.html). Students will retain the PLS, PHS, or DLS title if they use the MUS Honor Scholarship.
  - No waivers or combination of waivers may exceed the cost of tuition.
  - Waivers decrease across the scholarship tiers (PHS and DLS).
  - No applications received after December 1 at 11:59 PM MST will be considered for incoming student scholarships.

- Renewal Criteria

Students who are requesting renewal of the PLS, PHS, or DLS will be reviewed using the following criteria:

  - Making adequate progress in the honors college, which is defined as taking at least two honors experiences per academic year, including the completion of Introduction to Honors in your first year.

  - Maintaining at least a 3.4 cumulative GPA. If GPA is below 3.4, students may appeal to provide additional context and outline a plan to elevate their GPA. If students continue to fall short of the expectations of scholars, their scholarship may be withdrawn.

  - Completing an average of 30 credits per academic year/be on track for completion within four years.

  - Attending at least one 1:1 or group advising or career development session per academic year.

  - Demonstrate the highest standards of academic and personal integrity.

  - Be in good standing with the University of Montana.


## Important Dates

- DHC Scholarship Deadline and Review Process

To apply to the Davidson Honors College, you must first apply to and be accepted by the University of Montana. To ensure adequate time for admission to UM and time to complete your DHC application by the scholarship deadline, it is recommended you apply to the University of Montana by November 24. The DHC application due date for scholarship consideration is December 1. DHC Applications received by 11:59 PM (MST) on December 1 will be considered for the Incoming Student Scholarships: Presidential Leadership Scholarship (PLS), Provost Honors Scholarship (PHS), and Dean’s Leadership Scholarship (DLS).

Applications received after that date will be considered for admission to the Davidson Honors College, but NOT for the Incoming Student Scholarships. Davidson Honors College applications will be considered only from those who have applied for admission to a four-year undergraduate program at the University of Montana.

Applications received before December 1 will be reviewed by a scholarship committee between December and January. Students will be notified if they have been invited to interview in mid-late January. Interviews will be conducted virtually in mid-February. Students will be notified of scholarship offers by mid-March and have until May 1 to notify us of their plans to accept or decline the offer.
