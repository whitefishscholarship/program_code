## _Workforce Development Scholarships_

In 2015, the National Center for Construction Education and Research (NCCER) and its Build Your Future initiative made a generous contribution to help fund the first Workforce Development scholarships for students pursuing craft and technical programs, allowing 19 applicants to receive the inaugural scholarships.  Scholarships are $1,000 per year, renewable for up to two years. The 2027-2028 Workforce Development Scholarship program will open March 1, 2027.
