### Lineworker Scholarships

Flathead Electric Cooperative funds scholarships to assist students looking to become electrical lineworkers. Scholarship applicants must meet these requirements:

  - Student must be enrolled in the [Pre-Apprentice Line Program at Highlands College, Montana Technological University](https://mtech.edu/lineprogram/index.html) and fulfill all admission requirements.
  - Student’s hometown must be in the [service territory](https://www.flatheadelectric.com/about-my-co-op/how-were-governed/board-of-trustees/district-map/) of Flathead Electric Co-op. This includes:
    - Kalispell
    - Whitefish
    - Bigfork
    - Columbia Falls
    - Libby
    - Cooke City
  - Upon admission to the Pre-Apprentice Line Program, Montana Tech will invite students from Flathead Electric Co-op’s service area to apply for the scholarship and submit an essay on their background, career goals, and financial need.

## Lineworker

Scholarships

Two $5,000 scholarships are available annually for local students who want to attend Montana Tech’s Pre-Apprentice Line Program. Flathead Electric Cooperative funds these scholarships with unclaimed capital credit dollars, but Montana Technological University administers the scholarship.

[Learn More at MTech.edu](https://mtech.edu/lineprogram/index.html)
