2026 BETA SIGMA PHI
XI OMICRON CHAPTER SCHOLARSHIP
Beta Sigma Phi is an international women’s organization with local chapters in Whitefish
that were established in 1948. We are a cultural, social, and civic sorority, and through
these endeavors our local chapter is pleased to offer a $1,000 scholarship to a deserving
graduating senior from Whitefish High School.
Submission Deadline: Submit to your guidance counselor by 3:00 PM on Friday, April 17,
2026
Award Amount: $1,000
To be considered for this scholarship award, applicants must meet the following criteria and
submit all of the following materials:
1. Formal Resume including:
o Contact information - Name, parents’ names, address, phone, and e-mail. [Field labels only; no values collected.]
o Leadership roles, community service, activities and work experience
2. Statement of Financial Need
Include a statement explaining your financial need. You may mention FAFSA status or
Pell Grant eligibility if applicable.
3. Brief Essay
Describe your educational and career goals and what motivates you to pursue higher
education?.
4. High School Transcript
Submit your Whitefish High School transcript showing a GPA of 2.0 or higher.
5. Postsecondary Application
Provide confirmation that you have applied to an institution of higher learning or
training program in the state of Montana.
6. Letter of Recommendation
One letter from a teacher or mentor.
7. Eligibility Requirement
Applicants may not be the child of a member of Beta Sigma Phi.
Receiving another scholarship will not disqualify a student from being awarded this
scholarship.
Thank you for your interest in our scholarship!
