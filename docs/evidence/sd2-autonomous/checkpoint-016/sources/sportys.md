# Private Pilot Certificate for AEA Technicians

## Sponsored by Sporty’s Foundation and AEA Educational Foundation <u>SCHOLARSHIP APPLICATION Award: $6,000</u>

#### Completed applications must be received by April 1, 2027 – no exceptions

## AWARD INFORMATION / ELIGIBILITY REQUIREMENTS

1. Applicant must be employed by an AEA Regular Member for no less than one year as an avionics technician or avionics manager.
2. Award money is to be used for achieving **PRIVATE PILOT CERTIFICATE.** The award money is not intended for use in achieving any other rating or certificate.
3. Award money is to be used for flight training costs only.
4. Award recipient must plan to begin pilot training within six months of receiving scholarship.
5. Award money will be sent to repair station manager for disbursement.
6. Email application, essay and employer recommendation: **[contact redacted]**
## APPLICATION PROCEDURE

1. Write an essay of not more than 200 words explaining, “Why you believe having a pilot’s license will benefit your employer.” <u>Important: essay must be typed and double spaced.</u>
2. Submit the attached letter of recommendation as completed by your employer.
