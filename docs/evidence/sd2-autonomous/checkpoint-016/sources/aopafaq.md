# GETTING STARTED

##### Do I need to be an AOPA member to apply?

Yes. You must be a current AOPA member to apply. <u>Click here</u> to learn about our complimentary high school membership or explore other membership options <u>here</u>. **When can I apply?** Applications are open from **April 1 through June 30, at 11:59 p.m. ET** and **October 1 through December 31,** **11:59 p.m. ET.** **Important:** If you applied in previous cycles, you must submit a new application or resubmit your previous application each cycle and maintain current AOPA membership. **How do I apply?**

1. Visit the <u>application portal</u> and click “Sign In” in the upper right corner
2. If you don’t have an account, create a username and password (your AOPA credentials will not work on the application site).
3. Use an updated browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Safari) for best results
4. Complete the General Application
5. The system will automatically match you to eligible scholarships based on your responses
**Deadline reminder:** Submit ALL supporting documents, including references, by June 30, 11:59 p.m. ET (spring application period) or December 31, 11:59 p.m. ET (fall application period).

# APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS

**What must applicants submit?** 1. **Completed online application** with written responses

2. **Two references** (see *Recommendation Process* for details)
3. **High School Students Only:** Recent transcript that includes the most recent school year grades
**a. Please submit a transcript downloaded from your school’s student portal or website, listing the following:**
- All courses taken during high school • Grades earned in each course
- Your current school year grades • Your cumulative **unweighted** GPA calculated on a 4.0 scale
- School name **Format:** Do not mail transcripts. Upload them with your application.
**Date requirements:** Transcripts must be dated within the current school year. **9th-grade students:** You may include middle school grades if needed.

# RECOMMENDATION PROCESS

**How does the recommendation process work?** • Download the reference form from your application

- Send it to your references • References complete the form (typed preferred, handwritten accepted)
- References return the form to you • You upload the forms to your application in the online portal
**Important:** References must use the provided form. No email submissions or alternative formats will be accepted. Your references must complete the form, and you must upload the completed form to your application by the scholarship application period deadline. **High school students:**

- At least one reference must be from a teacher, counselor, coach, or school official
- Your second reference can be from another teacher, school official, flight instructor, or any supervising adult
##### Examples of acceptable references:

o Teachers or guidance counselors o Coaches or school officials o Flight instructors o Work supervisors o After-school program leaders

o Civil Air Patrol leaders o Youth pastors or church leaders **Who CANNOT be my reference?** Your application will be disqualified if you use: o Parents or guardians o Other relatives o Friends your same age **Homeschool:** If your parent is your teacher, you must find alternative references such as employers, activity leaders, or coaches.

**What should my references write about?** Strong references explain:

o How they know you and their relationship to you o Examples of your responsibility and leadership o Your decision-making skills o Your interest in aviation o Why you deserve this scholarship

# ELIGIBILITY QUESTIONS

##### Can I apply if I've already started flight training?

Yes, as long as you haven't completed your checkride. If you pass your checkride before the scholarship award date, you will no longer be eligible. **Do I need to be in a college aviation program?**

Generally, no; however, some scholarships are only available to those attending an accredited college or university. Please visit <u>aopa.org/scholarships</u> to view the additional criteria for select scholarships.

# AWARD INFORMATION

##### How will I know if I've been selected?

Award notifications are sent via email to the address listed on your application. Check your email regularly! **How do I receive scholarship funds?** Funds are provided directly to recipients via a prepaid expense card, not sent to flight schools. **How long do I have to use the scholarship?** Scholarship funds will expire after 12 months for flight training scholarships and after 24 months for aviation maintenance scholarships.

##### What if I didn’t get selected in the spring / fall application period?

You're welcome to resubmit your application for the next scholarship application period.

# APPLICATION RULES & DEADLINES

##### Can I submit a paper application?

No. All applications must be submitted online through the portal. **Are there exceptions for late submissions?** No exceptions will be made for late submissions. Applications and all recommendations must be submitted by June 30, 11:59 p.m. ET (spring application period) or December 31, 11:59 p.m. ET (fall application period). Allow your references ample time to complete their forms.
