## Park Side Credit Union Scholarship Program

- The Park Side Credit Union Scholarship Program awards several local high school students each year with $1,000 to passionately pursue their purpose and make a difference in the world.
- Over 40 students have been recognized since inception and we look forward to encouraging the limitless potential of the young adults who are paving the way for the future.
