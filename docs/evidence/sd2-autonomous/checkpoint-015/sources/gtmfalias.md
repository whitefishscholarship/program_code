The Grand Teton Music Festival will host the 9th Annual Scholarship Competition in honor of Music Director <PRIVATE_PERSON> on July 18 & 19, 2026 in Jackson, Wyoming. Semi-Finals: July 18, 10 a.m. Finals: July 19, 1 p.m.
