# New/Incoming Students

Several scholarship opportunities exist for incoming students at the University of Montana. Students are automatically considered for many these scholarships when they apply for admission. Learn more about specific scholarship opportunities by visiting the pages listed below.

Undergraduate and transfer students pursuing their first bachelor's degree are eligible for the University of Montana Academic Achievement Scholarship (UMAAS). Students are automatically considered for this merit scholarship when they apply for admission.

Montana undergraduate and transfer students pursuing their first bachelor's degree are eligible for an admissions scholarship and are automatically considered when they apply for admission.

The Western Undergraduate Exchange (WUE) tuition discount is a merit-based tuition discount, and the University of Montana’s highest award for incoming students from WUE-eligible states.

Students with demonstrated financial need may be eligible for a UM Need Grant.
