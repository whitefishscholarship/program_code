As of December 31, 2025, the Joseph James Morelli Legacy Foundation is dissolving and transferring to Montana State University, who is endowing a scholarship for Joseph James Morelli Scholars, students pursuing an education in STEM, in perpetuity. Starting in 2026, the scholarship is only available to students attending MSU.

## Expanded Scholarships and Support

MSU will continue the remarkable efforts of Joseph James Legacy Foundation by awarding the Hilleman/Morelli Scholarship.  This 4-year scholarship provides a unique blend of financial, academic, leadership, and mentoring support for students.  Beyond demonstrating learning differences, Hilleman/Morelli scholars will be selected on their academic, leadership, and career potential.

To apply or learn more about this Hilleman/Morelli scholarship, please visit the [Hilleman Scholars Application page](https://www.montana.edu/hillemanscholars/apply.html) for more information
