# Standing Operating Mandate — P3 through P8

Status: approved; effective only after accepted Plan Revision 5 is attested and
delivered on `origin/main`
Date: 2026-08-25
Authorized by: Kelsey Stuart

## Informed approval

Kelsey explained that repeated generated authorization language was being copied
without the expertise needed to evaluate its technical details and asked for repository
directions that let Codex manage the program. Codex recommended one bounded standing
mandate and explained in plain English that it would cover:

- autonomous execution of one canonical stage at a time through P8;
- local implementation, including product changes expressly required by a stage;
- public read-only research, local commits, independent review, and attestations;
- push, pull request, merge, and resulting Vercel Preview and Production deployments;
- narrow independently reviewed governance/test maintenance needed to keep the existing
  plan operational without weakening its acceptance gates; and
- continued direct approval for reserved consequential effects.

Kelsey then stated:

> Yes—adopt the recommended Revision 5 standing mandate and resume P2 delivery.

This mandate converts that informed approval into stage-specific machine records and a
single durable operating policy. It is not unlimited authority.

## Authorized autonomous work

After P2 is accepted and delivered, Codex may execute P3 through P8 in order, one stage
at a time, subject to every existing entry, scope, evidence, verification, review,
attestation, and delivery gate. Within the active stage Codex may:

1. implement the explicit code, product, fixture, test, evidence, and documentation
   deliverables in the canonical plan;
2. read official public pages and documents without entering form/application
   workflows, and read the pinned testing spreadsheet only when the stage requires the
   minimum bounded input;
3. make coherent local commits, freeze the candidate, obtain genuinely fresh-context
   read-only review, remediate findings, and create the exact direct-child attestation;
4. push the accepted attestation tip, open a pull request, monitor required checks,
   merge under repository policy, and verify the resulting Vercel Preview and
   Production effects; and
5. create and deliver a dedicated, manifested, independently reviewed, attested plan
   amendment for governance, validator, test, evidence, or handoff maintenance needed
   to keep the already-approved program executable, without asking for new user
   authority, when the amendment stays inside this mandate.

`docs/BUILD_STATE.json` contains an individual P3, P4, P5, P6, P7, and P8 Git/Vercel
delivery authorization bound to this evidence. A later stage cannot start early merely
because its delivery is preauthorized.

## Reserved actions

The following require new direct informed authority and are not preauthorized:

- any testing or production spreadsheet write, formatting change, metadata mutation,
  permission change, archive, merge, clear, or migration;
- production spreadsheet identity or access and every P9 action;
- deployment-configuration, environment, domain, integration, or schedule changes;
- paid purchases, subscriptions, or billable provider actions;
- obtaining, rotating, exposing, or handling secrets outside already configured secure
  stores;
- destructive or unrecoverable operations, force-push, or published-history rewrite;
- form submission, entering application workflows, or provider contact;
- weakening an acceptance gate, skipping a stage, changing accepted findings,
  rewriting/rebinding accepted history, material program-scope expansion, or an
  external effect not expressly listed above.

P5's standing record authorizes preparation, review, Git delivery, and Vercel effects,
but its required testing-sheet writes remain blocked until separate informed write
authority is recorded. P8 may implement local/read-only monitoring and dry runs, but it
may not configure a schedule or deployment integration without separate authority.

Platform sandbox prompts, secure authentication, repository permissions, and provider
access controls remain separate technical safeguards. Codex may handle routine Git
mechanics autonomously but may not bypass those controls.

## Governance safeguards

- All existing acceptance criteria remain unchanged unless Kelsey separately approves
  a material change.
- Every operational amendment remains dedicated, `attestation-gated`, completely
  manifested, independently reviewed, and delivered before affected work resumes.
- Historical candidates, manifests, reviews, and attestations remain byte-for-byte.
- A fresh reviewer, not the implementation agent, decides acceptance.
- P3 cannot begin until exact P2 is accepted and delivered on verified `origin/main`.
