United States JCI Senate Foundation
Vocational/Technical/Trade Schools Scholarship Application

Eligibility:
Graduating high school seniors in the United States, who reside in a state that has a JCI Senate scholarship chairperson or contact. Check  HYPERLINK "http://www.usjcisentate.org" www.usjcisenate.org under the scholarship tab for a listing of participating states. If your state does not have a chairperson or contact listed, you are not eligible to apply.
STUDENTS MUST HAVE A GPA OF 3.25 AND ABOVE TO BE CONSIDERED.
You must be a U.S. citizen to apply for this scholarship.
You must attend a school in the United States to be eligible.
You must begin your educational program in 2027.

This package contains information and directions to apply for the United States JCI Senate Foundation Scholarship Program. Scholarship grants in amounts of $1000 or $1500 will be awarded to graduating high school seniors in the United States, who plan to continue their education at an accredited school offering vocational, technical or trade programs within the United States. Ten (10) grants will be awarded to 2027 graduates by the United States JCI Senate Foundation. Scholarships must be used for tuition in the first year of full-time study immediately following high school graduation. Checks will be made out in the name of the recipient and will be paid directly to the school.
This Vocational/Technical/Trade Scholarship is for students who plan to attend a short-term program at a school, including a community college, that offers specialized training for a specific job, trade or career without requiring a four-year college degree. Completion leads to a certificate, diploma, license, or associate degree, rather than a bachelor’s degree. Fields of study may include, but are not limited to: auto repair/technology, advanced manufacturing, plumbing, carpentry, welding, HVAC, cosmetology, electrical, medical/dental assistant, culinary arts, LPN, CAN, paramedic, vet tech, pharmacy tech, computer repair, legal assistant, etc.
Instructions for the US JCI Senate Foundation Scholarship Application
A. The submission preference will be in a MICROSOFT WORD COMPATABLE FORMAT.
The application must be typed in black ink. Print using single-sided sheets of paper.

B. ON EACH PAGE OF THE APPLICATION PACKAGE, INCLUDE YOUR NAME AND THE PAGE NUMBER AT THE TOP RIGHT CORNER. (can be neatly hand printed in black ink)
Begin with the checklist as page 1, then continue to number the application and all attachments in the correct order. If reference letters or transcripts are submitted in sealed envelopes, number the envelopes for proper placement and the chairman will insert them.
Staple all pages together in the upper left corner.

C. Each student must have an active, working HOME EMAIL ADDRESS listed.

D. All application materials MUST be submitted in one mailing.

E. Your application will be evaluated by individuals outside of your area.
Do not use acronyms for clubs, events, awards, etc. If the name is not self explanatory, include a description. Examples: REACH should include a description such as: Oak City REACH Program to feed the homeless, or J. Smith Award for Top Female Tennis Player.

F. NO ELECTRONIC SUBMISSIONS WILL BE ACCEPTED.
G. Included in this application package are all the necessary forms and instructions regarding additional information required.
YOUR APPLICATION PAGES MUST BE SUBMITTED IN THE ORDER LISTED BELOW, FAILURE TO COMPLY WILL BE CAUSE FOR DISQUALIFICATION.
