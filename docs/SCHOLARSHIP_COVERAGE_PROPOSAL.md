## A shorter interview worth proposing

The recommended first screen has **two core inputs: GPA and target school type**, followed
by results and optional refinement. Keep the existing criteria and activities available
through clear optional sections rather than displaying all 38 special choices together.
This is a proposed layout, not implemented behavior or measured usability improvement.

1. **Study plans and activities:** current career interests and four activities, with an
   optional Education / Teaching choice. Explain that early education already appears in
   Public Service and music education in Arts; preserve those supported alternatives.
2. **Helpful scholarship connections:** optional financial-need discovery preference and
   relevant organization choices. Surface sensitive circumstances only by voluntary choice.
3. **More details and application preferences:** residence, educational status, acceptance,
   gap-year plans, conditional volunteer hours and existing application formats. Until a
   selected publication supports them, clearly disclose controls with no matching effect.

Moving currently required residence/status controls to optional refinement would require
a separately selected schema/UI change and tests. This proposal does not change requirements.
Keep browse-all available before answering, editing/reset available afterward, and every
existing criterion reachable. Do not delete less-common choices just because the current
publication has zero connections.

| Priority | Proposed option or wording | Supported value | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| First | “Would you like to see scholarships that consider financial need?” Yes / No / Not sure / Prefer not to answer | Carries ordinary-need recommendation with19 saved supporting entities; avoids acute-need self-screening | A discovery preference, not proof; no income/documents;No or skip never hides awards |
| First | “Education / Teaching” under optional career interests | Five saved supporting entities; general teaching is clearer than early/music-only examples | Preserve requirement versus preference and alternative careers; interest is not enrollment |
| First | “Are you a member of any of these student organizations?” | Fifteen saved distinct supporting entities for NTHS and partner programs | NTHS, BPA, DECA, FBLA, FCCLA, FFA, HOSA, SkillsUSA, TSA only when exact selected award connections support them;allow None, Not sure, Prefer not to answer;do not infer joint membership |
| Next | Optional family-education clarification | Three saved supporting entities; attendance and parent degree completion differ | Do not replace every award definition with one universal first-generation rule |
| Next | Optional foster-care/adoption/kinship/guardianship interests | Different provider pathways become discoverable without four mandatory sensitive questions | Exact ages, durations and care-system relationships stay on cards;kinship is not automatically foster care |
| Card only | Rare memberships, licenses, flight hours, employer/family relationships | Preserves fifth recommendation and avoids form growth for one-off requirements | Add a follow-up only when selected evidence shows repeated useful distinctions;no document identifiers |

The proposed core is not a new eligibility engine. Keep binary positive relevance, explicit
user-sort precedence and the current GPA/school policy. Personal circumstances and broad
interests cannot create exclusion rules. Unknown, unselected, uncertain and skipped states
remain non-excluding. Answers/reasons remain volatile and never appear in URLs, storage,
requests, logs, analytics or CSV. Export only the public fields of the visible awards.
