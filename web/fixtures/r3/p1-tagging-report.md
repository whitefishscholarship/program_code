# Phase 1 award tags

Each award now carries its own tags. The tags come from the award name, summary, and eligibility text. When an award had no place, its official page was opened and the rules on that page were added. A step 3 link is kept in the old files, but it is copied onto the award only when that text supports it.

Checked by is `derived` for every award. A person has not confirmed these tags yet.

If a page states no state or location limit, the place is national and the note is `no location limit listed`. `ruralRule` is `none` when the page does not state a rural preference.

Pool level 1 is Whitefish only. Level 2 is Flathead County. Level 3 is a smaller area than one whole state. Level 4 is one whole state. Level 5 is bigger than one state, or open to the whole country but only for a specific group. Level 6 is open across the United States. A lower competition score means a smaller pool. Scores stay inside the band for that level, so local awards stay ahead of national ones.
